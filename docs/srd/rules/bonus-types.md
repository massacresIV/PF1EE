# Bonus Types

## Alchemical
- **Description:** An alchemical bonus is granted by the use of a non-magical, alchemical substance such as antitoxin.
- **Stackable:** Does not stack.

## Dodge
- **Description:** A dodge bonus improves armor class (and sometimes Reflex saves) resulting from physical skill at avoiding blows and other ill effects. Dodge bonuses are usually not granted by spells or magic items. Any situation or effect (except wearing armor) that negates a character’s Dexterity bonus also negates any dodge bonuses the character may have. Dodge bonuses apply against touch attacks.
- **Stackable:** Dodge bonuses stack with all other bonuses to AC, even other dodge bonuses.

## Natural Armor
- **Description:** A natural armor bonus improves armor class resulting from a creature’s naturally tough hide. Some magical effects (such as the barkskin spell) grant an enhancement bonus to the creature’s existing natural armor bonus, which has the effect of increasing the natural armor’s overall bonus to armor class. A natural armor bonuses do not apply against touch attacks.
- **Stackable:** Natural armor bonuses stack with all other bonuses to AC, even other natural armor bonuses.
