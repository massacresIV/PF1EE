# Conditions

## Fascinated
A fascinated creature is entranced by a supernatural or spell effect. The creature can take no actions other than to pay attention to the source of the fascinating effect, for as long as the effect lasts. It does not threaten squares and cannot make attacks of opportunity. The creature takes a –4 penalty on skill checks made as reactions, such as Perception checks.

The condition ends immediately if the fascinated creature is attacked, takes damage, or is otherwise directly threatened. An ally may break the effect as a standard action through abrupt physical contact, such as shaking or slapping the creature. Otherwise, the creature may attempt a new saving throw at the end of each of its turns if it becomes aware of nearby danger through its senses. When combat is clearly occurring nearby, the creature receives a +2 circumstance bonus on all saving throws against the fascinating effect, including the initial saving throw.

Fascinate is a mind-affecting condition.

:::info[Changes]
No longer grants additional saving throws for potential threats. No longer automatically ends when seeing obvious threats. Creatures now gain a +2 circumstance bonus on saves if combat is clearly occurring nearby.
