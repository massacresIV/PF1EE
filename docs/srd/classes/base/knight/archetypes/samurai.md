# Samurai

---

## Weapon and Armor Proficiency
Samurai are proficient with all simple and martial weapons, plus the katana, naginata, nodachi, and wakizashi. Samurai are proficient with all types of armor (heavy, light, and medium) and shields (except tower shields).

---

## Iaijutsu Technique (Ex) 
At 3rd level, a samurai gains unparalleled speed and precision in combat. He gains *Quick Draw* as a bonus feat, even if he does not meet the prerequisites. In addition, the samurai gains a +2 bonus on all critical confirmation rolls made with melee or ranged weapons. Finally, his samurai levels as fighter levels for the purpose of qualifying for feats that select a weapon group (as per the fighter’s weapon training class feature), such as Weapon Specialization.

<u>This ability replaces Knight's Charge.</u>

---

## Mounted Archer (Ex)
At 4th level, the samurai becomes skilled at firing ranged weapons while mounted. A samurai only takes a –2 penalty on attack rolls with ranged weapons while his mount takes a double move. This penalty increases to –4 while his mount is running.

<u>This ability replaces Expert Trainer.</u>
