# Gendarme
*The gendarme cares less for the finer points of tactical precision than he does for the exhilaration of the charge: the rush of wind through the visor of his helmet, the feel of his couched lance, the satisfying shriek of armor giving way before his weapon’s force as the point drives past metal into his foes.*

---

## Gendarme's Bond (Ex)
At 1st level, a Gendarme must choose a Mount as his Knight’s bond, not a Weapon Bond.

---

## Gendarme Bonus Feats
A Gendarme trains to be a mounted terror, almost to the exclusion of all other abilities. At 1st level, 3rd level, and every three levels thereafter, a Gendarme gains a bonus feat in addition to those gained from normal advancement. These bonus feats must be selected from those listed as combat feats.
:::info[Changes]
Adjusted feat intervals to avoid dead levels. The Gendarme can now select from all combat feats, rather than a limited list that could softlock itself. 
:::
<u>This ability replaces Tactician, Greater Tactician, Master Tactician, and alters the knight’s bonus feat progression.</u>

---

## Transfixing Charge (Ex)
At 20th level, a Gendarme represents the epitome of Mounted Combat. Whenever he makes a charge attack while mounted, he deals triple the normal damage (quadruple if using a lance); this damage includes all increases from the Spirited Charge feat and from the use of a lance. In addition, if the gendarme confirms a critical hit on a charge attack while mounted, the attack deals maximum damage for the weapon wielded. Additional damage from weapon properties, magic effects, precision-based bonuses, or other increases are rolled normally.

<u>This ability replaces supreme charge.</u>
