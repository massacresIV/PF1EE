Mounted or on foot, the Knight is a brave warrior, using his wit, charm, and strength at arms to rally his companions and achieve his goals.

While many warriors strive to perfect their art, spending all of their time honing their skill at martial arms, others spend as much effort dedicating themselves to a cause. These warriors, known as Knights, swear themselves to a purpose, serving it above all else. Knights are skilled at fighting from horseback, and are often found charging across a battlefield, with the symbol of their order trailing on a long, fluttering banner. The Knight’s true power comes from the conviction of his ideals, the oaths that he swears, and the challenges he makes.

**Role:** Knights tend to marshal forces on the battlefield, using their mounted talents and challenges to control the flow of the fight. Outside of battle, Knights can be found advancing their cause through diplomacy and, if needed, subterfuge. The Knight is no stranger to courtly intrigue and can hold his own in even the most delicate of social situations.

**Alignment:** Any.

**Hit Die:** d12.

**Starting Wealth:** 5d6 × 10 gp (average 175 gp.) In addition, each character begins play with an outfit worth 10 gp or less.

# Class Skills
The Knight’s class skills are Bluff (Cha), Climb (Str), Craft (Int), Diplomacy (Cha), Handle Animal (Cha), Intimidate (Cha), Profession (Wis), Ride (Dex), Sense Motive (Wis), and Swim (Str).

**Skill Points at each Level:** 4 + Int modifier.

## Weapon and Armor Proficiency
Knights are proficient with all simple and martial weapons, with all types of armor (heavy, light, and medium) and with shields (except tower shields).

## Challenge (Ex)
Once per day, a Knight can Challenge a foe to combat. As a Swift Action, the Knight chooses one target within sight to challenge. The Knight’s melee attacks deal extra damage whenever the attacks are made against the target of his Challenge. This extra damage is equal to the Knight’s level. The Knight can use this ability once per day at 1st level, plus one additional time per day for every three levels beyond 1st, to a maximum of seven times per day at 19th level.

Challenging a foe requires much of the Knight’s concentration. The Knight takes a –2 penalty to his Armor Class, except against attacks made by the target of his Challenge.

The Challenge remains in effect until the target is dead or unconscious or until the combat ends. Each Knight’s Challenge also includes another effect which is listed in the section describing the Knight’s Order.

## Knight's Bond
At 1st level, Knights form a powerful bond. This bond can take one of two forms: a Mount or a Weapon Bond. A mount is a trusty that enhances the Knight’s mobility, share burdens, and can carry him great distances. While a Weapon is a weapon becomes an extension of the Knight's body, allowing the weapon to adopt unique properties. Once a Knight makes this choice, it is permanent and cannot be changed.

### Weapon Bond (Su)
Knights who select a Weapon Bond begins play with this weapon at no cost. This weapon is always masterwork quality. His bond allows the Knight to manipulate the weapon’s material composition and, as they grow stronger, enhance its combat potential.

As a swift action, the Knight can alter the material composition of their bonded weapon to a single material, allowing him to bypass damage reduction as if it were adamantine, cold iron, silver, or any other common material required to overcome DR. This effect lasts for 1 minute. At 4th level, this power grants the weapon a +1 enhancement bonus. For every 4 levels beyond 4th, this bonus increases by 1 (to a maximum of +5 at 20th level). These bonuses stack with any existing bonuses the weapon might have, to a maximum of +5. The Knight can enhance a weapon with any of the following weapon special abilities: corrosive, defending, flaming, frost, keen, and shock. Adding any of these special abilities replaces an amount of bonus equal to the special ability’s base cost. Duplicate abilities do not stack. The weapon must have at least a +1 enhancement bonus before any other special abilities can be added. A Knight can use this ability once per day at 1st level, and one additional time per day for every four levels thereafter, to a total of five times per day at 17th level.

If a bonded weapon is damaged, it is restored to full hit points the next time the Knight rests for 8 hours or more. If a bonded weapon lost or destroyed, the Knight finds a suitable replacement after 1 week of searching, paying a fee equal to the cost of the chosen masterwork weapon. A Knight can designate an existing masterwork weapon as his Bonded Weapon. This functions in the same way as replacing a lost or destroyed weapon except he does not need to pay the cost of a new weapon.

:::info[Changes]
Alternative option for non-mounted characters.
:::

### Mount (Ex)
A Knight gains the service of a loyal and trusty steed to carry him into battle. This mount functions as a druid’s animal companion, using the Knight’s level as his effective druid level. The creature must be one that he is capable of riding and is suitable as a mount. This mount may come from the following list:

**Medium Knight:** Axe Beak, Camel, Elk, Giraffe, Horse, Giant Seahorse, Giant Tortoise, Zebra.

**Small Knight:** Antelope, Boar, Capybara, Dog, Kangaroo, Lizard (Giant Gecko), Pony, Ram, Reindeer, Stag, Weasel, Giant, Wolf, Wolfdog.

The GM might approve other animals as suitable mounts.

A Knight does not take an armor check penalty on Ride checks while riding his mount. The mount is always considered combat trained and begins play with Light Armor Proficiency as a bonus feat. A Knight’s mount does not gain the share spells special ability.

A Knight’s bond with his mount is strong, with the pair learning to anticipate each other’s moods and moves. Should a Knight’s mount die, the Knight may find another mount to serve him after 1 week of mourning. This new mount does not gain the link, evasion, devotion, or improved evasion special abilities until the next time the Knight gains a level or spends at least 1 week with his new mount, whichever comes sooner.

:::info[Changes]
Now regain animal companion features after spending 1 week with a new mount, rather than having to wait until level up.
:::

## Order (Ex)
At 1st level, a Knight must pledge himself to a specific Order. The Order grants the Knight a number of bonuses, class skills, and special abilities. In addition, each Order includes a number of edicts that the Knight must follow. If he violates any of these edicts, he loses the benefits from his order’s challenge ability for 24 hours. The violation of an edict is subject to GM interpretation.

A Knight cannot change his Order without undertaking a lengthy process to dedicate himself to a new cause. When this choice is made, he immediately loses all of the benefits from his old order. He must then follow the edicts of his new Order for one entire level or one month without gaining any benefits from that Order, whichever comes sooner. Once accomplished, he gains all of the bonuses from his new order. Note that the names of these orders might vary depending upon the campaign setting or GM’s preference.

:::info[Changes]
Now able to change Orders by following edicts for up to a month without benefit.
:::

## Tactician (Ex)
At 1st level, a Knight receives a teamwork feat as a bonus feat. He must meet the prerequisites for this feat. As a Standard Action, the Knight can grant one of his teamwork feats to all allies within 30 feet who can see and hear him. Allies retain the use of this bonus feat for 1 minute. Allies do not need to meet the prerequisites of these bonus feats. The Knight can use this ability once per day at 1st level, plus one additional time per day at 5th level and for every 5 levels thereafter.

:::info[Changes]
Now allows the Knight to share any Teamwork feat he possesses, not just the Teamwork feat you chose. Duration changed from 3 + 1/2 Knight to a 1 minute.
:::

## Knight's Charge (Ex)
At 3rd level, a Knight learns to make more accurate charge attacks. The Knight receives a +4 bonus on melee attack rolls made as part of a charge (instead of the normal +2). In addition, the Knight does not suffer any penalty to his AC after making a charge attack.

:::info[Changes]
Now also applies to unmounted charges.
:::

## Expert Trainer (Ex)
At 4th level, a Knight learns to train mounts with speed and unsurpassed expertise. The Knight receives a bonus equal to 1/2 his Knight level whenever he uses Handle Animal on an animal that serves as a mount. In addition, he can reduce the time needed to teach a mount a new trick or train a mount for a general purpose to 1 day per 1 week required by increasing the DC by +5. He can also train more than one mount at once, although each mount after the first adds +2 to the DC.

## Knight's Standard (Ex)
At 5th level, a Knight's Standard becomes a symbol of inspiration to his allies and companions. The Knight or his mount may wear a tabard, sashimono, carry a flag, or other personal insignia, which represents their standard. As long as the Knight’s Standard is clearly visible, all allies within 60 feet receive a bonus depending on the Knight's Standard. The Knight may choose one of the following Standards to bear;

### Fleet Standard (Ex)
An ally who benefits from this standard increases her land speed by 10 feet and gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC. This bonus to speed is an enhancement bonus. At 10th level and every 5 levels thereafter, the bonus to speed increases by 10 feet.

At 14th level, this becomes a Greater Knight's Standard, granting additional bonuses to the Knight and his allies. An ally benefiting from the Greater Fleet Standard gains a +2 morale bonus on Reflex saving throws against effects that target an area (such as fireball or breath weapons). The Knight also gains the ability to rally the attention of his allies using his standard. As a Standard Action, each ally within 60 feet can move up to her speed as an Immediate Action. An ally cannot benefit from this ability more than once per day.
:::info[Changes]
Increased the Movement speed increase by 5ft. at all intervals. Dodge bonus to AC is now flat.
:::

### Knave Standard (Ex)
An ally who benefits from this standard gains a +1 bonus on caster level checks to bypass spell resistance and a +1 bonus to the DC of her hexes and necromancy or enchantment spell she casts. At 10th level and every 5 levels thereafter, the bonus on caster level checks increases by 1.

At 14th level, this becomes a Greater Knight's Standard, granting additional bonuses to the Knight and his allies. An ally benefiting from the Greater Jinx Standard gains a +2 morale bonus on saving throws against spells or effects that deal hit point damage. The Knight also gains the ability to rally the attention of his allies using his standard. As a Standard Action, a number of allies equal to the Knight's Charisma modifier (minimum 1) within 60 feet can attempt an Intimidate check to demoralize a foe as an immediate action. An ally cannot benefit from this ability more than once per day.

### Stalwart Standard (Ex)
An ally who benefits from this standard gains a +2 morale bonus to CMD and DR 1/-. At 10th level and every 5 levels thereafter, the bonus to CMD and the Damage Reduction increase by +1. This Damage Reduction stacks with DR gained from feats and materials, but not from other class features.

At 14th level, this becomes a Greater Knight's Standard, granting additional bonuses to the Knight and his allies. An ally benefiting from the Greater Stalwart Standard is treated as one size category larger for the purposes of determining which creatures can affect him with combat maneuvers. The Knight also gains the ability to rally the attention of his allies using his standard. As a Standard Action, each ally within 60 feet can move up to 10 feet without provoking attacks of opportunities and ignoring difficult terrain as an immediate action. An ally cannot benefit from this ability more than once per day.
:::info[Changes]
Now grants DR instead of a non-scaling bonus to attacks.
:::

### Valor Standard (Ex)
An ally who benefits from this standard gains a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls, weapon damage rolls, and saving throws against fear effects. At 10th level, and every five levels thereafter, these bonuses increase by +1.

At 14th level, this becomes a Greater Knight's Standard, granting additional bonuses to the Knight and his allies. An ally benefiting from the Greater Valor Standard also gains the standard's current morale bonus on saving throws against charm and compulsion effects. The Knight also gains the ability to rally the attention of his allies using his standard. As a Standard Action, granting all allies within 60 feet an additional saving throw against any one spell or effect that is targeting them. This save is made at the original DC. Spells and effects that do not allow saving throws are unaffected by this ability. An ally cannot benefit from this ability more than once per day.
:::info[Changes]
Now applies to all attacks, not just charge attacks.
:::

## Knight Feats
At 6th level, and at every six levels thereafter, a Knight gains a bonus feat in addition to those gained from normal advancement. These bonus feats must be selected from those listed as combat feats. The Knight must meet the prerequisites of these bonus feats.

## Greater Tactician (Ex)
At 9th level, the Knight receives an additional teamwork feat as a bonus feat. He must meet the prerequisites for this feat. Additionally, using the Tactician ability becomes a Swift Action.

:::info[Changes]
Language adjusted to account for Tactician.
:::

## Mighty Charge (Ex)
At 11th level, a Knight learns to make devastating charge attacks. Double the threat range of any weapons wielded during a charge. This increase does not stack with other effects that increase the threat range of the weapon. In addition, the Knight can make a free bull rush, disarm, sunder, or trip combat maneuver if his charge attack is successful. This free combat maneuver does not provoke an attack of opportunity.

:::info[Changes]
Now also applies to unmounted charges.
:::

## Demanding Challenge (Ex)
At 12th level, whenever a Knight declares a challenge, his target must pay attention to the threat he poses. As long as the target is within the threatened area of the Knight, it takes a –2 penalty to its AC from attacks made by anyone other than the Knight.

## Master Tactician (Ex)
At 17th level, the Knight receives an additional teamwork feat as a bonus feat. He must meet the prerequisites for this feat. Whenever the Knight uses the Tactician ability, he grants any two teamwork feats that he knows.

:::info[Changes]
Language adjusted to account for Tactician.
:::

## Supreme Charge (Ex)
At 20th level, whenever the Knight makes a charge attack, he deals double the normal amount of damage (or triple if using a lance whilst mounted). In addition, if the Knight confirms a critical hit on a charge attack, the target is stunned for 1d4 rounds. A Will save reduces this to staggered for 1d4 rounds. The DC is equal to 10 + the Knight’s base attack bonus.

:::info[Changes]
Now also applies to unmounted charges.
:::
