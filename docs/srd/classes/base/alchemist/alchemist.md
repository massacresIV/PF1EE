# Alchemist
<figure className="artworkFigure">
  <img src="/PF1EE/img/artwork/classes/Alchemist.png" alt="Alchemist Class Artwork" />
  <figcaption className="artworkCaption">Art by Wayne Reynolds</figcaption>
</figure>
The alchemist is the master of alchemy, using extracts to grant him great power, mutagens to enhance his form, and bombs to destroy his enemies.

Whether secreted away in a smoky basement laboratory or gleefully experimenting in a well-respected school of magic, the alchemist is often regarded as being just as unstable, unpredictable, and dangerous as the concoctions he brews. While some creators of alchemical items content themselves with sedentary lives as merchants, providing tindertwigs and smokesticks, the true alchemist answers a deeper calling. Rather than cast magic like a spellcaster, the alchemist captures his own magic potential within liquids and extracts he creates, infusing his chemicals with virulent power to grant him impressive skill with poisons, explosives, and all manner of self-transformative magic.

**Role:** The alchemist’s reputation is not softened by his exuberance (some would say dangerous recklessness) in perfecting his magical extracts and potion-like creations, infusing these substances with magic siphoned from his aura and using his own body as experimental stock. Nor is it mollified by the alchemist’s almost gleeful passion for building explosive bombs and discovering strange new poisons and methods for their use. These traits, while making him a liability and risk for most civilized organizations and institutions of higher learning, seem to fit quite well with most adventuring groups.

**Alignment:** Any.

**Hit Die:** d8.

**Starting Wealth:** 3d6 × 10 gp (average 105gp.) In addition, each character begins play with an outfit worth 10 gp or less.

# Class Skills
The alchemist’s class skills are Appraise (Int), Craft (any) (Int), Disable Device (Dex), Fly (Dex), Heal (Wis), Knowledge (arcana) (Int), Knowledge (nature) (Int), Perception (Wis), Profession (Wis), Sleight of Hand (Dex), Spellcraft (Int), Survival (Wis), Use Magic Device (Cha).

**Skill Ranks per Level:** 4 + Int modifier.

## Alchemy (Su)
An alchemist is a master of crafting alchemical substances such as bombs, mutagens, and magical extracts that function like spells in potion form. Unlike traditional spellcasters, the alchemist does not cast spells but instead prepares extracts by infusing ingredients with magical power drawn from his own aura.

When using Profession (Alchemy) to create an alchemical item, an alchemist gains a competence bonus equal to his class level on the Profession (Alchemy) check. In addition, an alchemist can use Profession (Alchemy) to identify potions as if using detect magic. He must hold the potion for 1 round to make such a check.

Though the alchemist does not cast spells, he has a formulae list that governs which extracts he can create. He can activate spell-trigger magic items for spells on his formulae list, but cannot use spell-completion items unless he succeeds at a Use Magic Device check.

To create or use an extract, the alchemist must have an Intelligence score equal to at least 10 + the extract’s level. The saving throw DC against an alchemist’s extract is 10 + the extract’s level + the alchemist’s Intelligence modifier.

An alchemist can create only a limited number of extracts per extract level each day, as shown on the class progression table. In addition, he receives bonus extracts per day if he has a high Intelligence score, in the same way a wizard receives bonus spells per day (see “Ability Modifiers and Bonus Spells”).

An extract is “cast” by drinking it, producing effects identical to the spell it is based on. The alchemist can consume any of his own extracts as a standard action, regardless of whether they are infused. Allies can only benefit from infused extracts and must likewise drink them as a standard action to gain their effects. Drinking an extract functions like drinking a potion and provokes attacks of opportunity. The alchemist’s caster level for extracts equals his alchemist level.

<details>
<summary><strong>Rest and Extract Preparation</strong></summary>

To prepare extracts, an alchemist must meet all the following requirements:

- **Rest:** The alchemist must rest for 8 hours to recover extracts. This rest must be free from combat, movement, spellcasting, or other demanding activity. Interruptions add 1 hour per disruption, and he must have at least 1 uninterrupted hour before preparing extracts.

- **No Recent Use:** Any extracts imbibed in the last 8 hours count against the alchemist’s daily extract allotment. For example, consuming a 3rd-level extract before resting reduces that day’s 3rd-level extracts available to create by one.

- **Formula Book:** The alchemist must refer to his formula book when preparing extracts, but not when using them. He can prepare any extract recorded in his formula book.

- **Preparation Environment:** The alchemist’s surroundings must be reasonably quiet and free of distractions to concentrate. Inclement weather, injury, or failing saving throws can prevent preparation. The alchemist must have access to his formula book and sufficient light.

- **Partial Preparation and Open Extract Slots:** The alchemist can prepare some extracts at a time, requiring 1 minute per extract to mix ingredients and infuse them with magical power. He may leave extract slots open and later prepare extracts in those slots with at least 1 minute of work. However, he cannot replace or refill slots already used without resting again.

- **Prepared Extracts:** When preparing an extract, the alchemist can choose to infuse it with a portion of his magical power. An infused extract remains active even when not in the alchemist’s possession and can be consumed by allies to gain its effects. While it exists, an infused extract continues to occupy one of the alchemist’s daily extract slots. If the alchemist chooses not to infuse an extract, it remains potent for 1 day but becomes inert if it leaves his possession, reactivating only when returned to him.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Formula Book and Gaining Formulae</strong></summary>

The alchemist stores known formulae in a formula book, which he consults to prepare extracts.

- **Starting Formula Book:** At 1st level, the alchemist’s formula book contains two 1st-level formulae of his choice plus additional formulae equal to his Intelligence modifier.

- **Gaining New Formulae:** Each time the alchemist gains a new level, he adds two new formula of any level he can prepare to his formula book.

- **Adding Formulae:** The alchemist can add formulae to his book by copying them from other formula books or spellbooks, using the same rules, costs, and times as a wizard copying spells. He can also learn formulae from scrolls and magical writings without needing to decipher arcane scripts.

- **Exclusive Learning:** Wizards cannot learn spells from an alchemist’s formula book.

</details>

## Greater Mutagen (Ex)
At 12th level, when the alchemist creates a mutagen, he enhances its potency. His mutagen now grants a +6 alchemical bonus to one physical ability score (Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution), a +4 alchemical bonus to a second physical ability score, and a +4 natural armor bonus.

While the mutagen persists, the alchemist takes a –2 penalty to both mental ability scores associated with the affected physical abilities (for example, a Strength mutagen imposes a penalty to Intelligence and Charisma).

## Grand Mutagen (Ex)
At 16th level, the alchemist’s mutagen becomes even more potent. When he creates a mutagen, it grants a +8 alchemical bonus to one physical ability score (Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution), a +6 alchemical bonus to a second physical ability score, a +4 alchemical bonus to a third physical ability score, and a +6 natural armor bonus.

While the mutagen persists, the alchemist takes a –2 penalty to Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma.
