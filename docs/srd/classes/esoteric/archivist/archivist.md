# Archivist
<figure className="artworkFigure">
  <img src="/PF1EE/img/artwork/classes/Archivist.png" alt="Archivist Class Artwork" />
  <figcaption className="artworkCaption">Art by Eric Polak</figcaption>
</figure>

A seeker of sacred knowledge, the archivist wields divine magic with the precision and scholarship of an academic mind.

In sacred texts and the relics of forgotten faiths, archivists uncover divine power divorced from dogma, wielding it through intellect and study rather than prayer. Equal parts theologian and scholar, an archivist prepares divine spells from written lore, often salvaged from ruins or ancient scriptures. Though their magic mirrors that of priests, their pursuit of forbidden knowledge and esoteric miracles sets them apart from traditional clergy—earning both reverence and suspicion. Walking the line between reverence and reason, they defend themselves with wisdom and will, bolstering allies with insight while unraveling secrets that others fear to name.

**Role:** Archivists are versatile divine spellcasters who prepare their spells from written texts, much like a wizard. Unlike clerics, they are not bound to a single deity or dogma, allowing them to learn and prepare divine spells from any source they can transcribe. They excel in parties that rely on foreknowledge, support, and healing, and they shine brightest when confronting foes with hidden strengths or obscure weaknesses. Though less durable than front-line warriors, archivists bolster their allies and unravel their enemies with divine insight and academic cunning.

**Alignment:** Any.

**Hit Die:** d6.

**Starting Wealth:** 2d6 × 10 gp (average 70 gp.) In addition, each character begins play with an outfit worth 10 gp or less.

# Class Skills

**Skill Points at each Level:** 4 + Int modifier.

## Archivist Spellcasting

An Archivist casts divine spells drawn from the cleric spell list. Unlike clerics, an Archivist prepares spells from a prayerbook, a collection of copied divine spells. He must choose and prepare his spells ahead of time by resting and studying his prayerbook.

To learn, prepare, or cast a spell, an Archivist must have an Intelligence score equal to 10 + the spell’s level. The saving throw DC for an Archivist’s spell is 10 + the spell’s level + the Archivist’s Intelligence modifier.

An Archivist can only cast a limited number of spells per spell level each day, as shown on the class progression table. In addition, he receives bonus spells per day if his Intelligence is high enough (see “Ability Modifiers and Bonus Spells”).

<details>
<summary><strong>Rest and Spell Preparation</strong></summary>

To prepare spells, an Archivist must meet all the following conditions:

- **Rest:** The Archivist must rest for 8 hours to recover spell slots. This rest must be free from combat, movement, spellcasting, or other demanding activity. Each interruption adds 1 hour of rest required. The Archivist must have at least 1 uninterrupted hour immediately prior to preparing spells.

- **No Recent Casting:** Any spells cast in the last 8 hours count against the number of spells the Archivist can prepare. For example, casting a 5th-level spell before resting reduces that day’s 5th-level spell slots available for preparation by one.

- **Study:** After resting, the Archivist spends up to 1 hour studying his prayerbook to prepare spells. He chooses which spells to prepare and how many times each is prepared, up to his remaining spell slots.

- **Preparation Environment:** The Archivist’s surroundings must be reasonably quiet and free of distractions to concentrate. Inclement weather, injury, or failing saving throws during study can prevent preparation. The Archivist must have access to his prayerbook and sufficient light, except he can prepare *read magic* from memory without the prayerbook.

- **Partial Preparation and Open Slots:** The Archivist can prepare only some spells at a time, requiring at least 15 minutes. He may leave spell slots empty and later prepare spells in those slots by studying for at least 15 minutes. However, he cannot replace or refill slots already used without resting again.

- **Prepared Spells:** Prepared spells remain in the Archivist’s mind until cast or abandoned. If the Archivist dies, all prepared spells are lost.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Prayerbook and Gaining Spells</strong></summary>

The Archivist’s prayerbook functions like a wizard’s spellbook but contains divine spells instead of arcane ones.

- **Starting Prayerbook:** At 1st level, the Archivist’s prayerbook contains all 0-level cleric spells plus three 1st-level cleric spells of choice, plus additional 1st-level spells equal to his Intelligence modifier.

- **Gaining New Spells:** Each time the Archivist gains a new level, he adds two new cleric spells of any level he can cast to his prayerbook.

- **Copying Spells:** The Archivist can copy spells from any prayerbook, spellbook, or spell scroll, as long as the spell is on the Cleric spell list:
  - **Deciphering:** Firstly deciper the magical writing (Spellcraft DC 20 + spell level, or use *read magic*).
  - **Studying:** Secondly after a successful deciphering, study the spell for 1 hour, then succeeding at a Spellcraft check (DC 15 + spell level). Specialists receive +2 on this check for spells from their specialty school.
  - **Copying:** Thirdly after a successful study, copying the spell to the prayerbook, which costs 1 hour per spell level (30 minutes for 0-level spells), one page per spell level, and materials costing gold equal to the spell level’s write cost.

    | Spell Level | Cost |
    |-------------|------|
    | 0           | 5 gp |
    | 1           | 10 gp |
    | 2           | 40 gp |
    | 3           | 90 gp |
    | 4           | 160 gp |
    | 5           | 250 gp |
    | 6           | 360 gp |
    | 7           | 490 gp |
    | 8           | 640 gp |
    | 9           | 810 gp |

- **Independent Research:** Archivists may research new spells independently, taking at least 1 week and 1,000 gp per spell level plus relevant Spellcraft and Knowledge (arcana) checks at GM discretion.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Borrowed Prayerbooks and Replacing Prayerbooks</strong></summary>

- An Archivist can prepare spells he already knows from a borrowed prayerbook but must:
  - Decipher the writing (Spellcraft DC 20 + spell level, or *read magic*).
  - Succeed on a Spellcraft check (DC 15 + spell level) each time he prepares that spell from the borrowed prayerbook.

- If the Archivist loses his prayerbook, he can reconstruct it by:
  - Writing prepared spells into a new prayerbook at the normal cost, erasing them from his mind.
  - Preparing spells from a borrowed prayerbook to copy them.
  - Duplicating a prayerbook halves the time and cost to copy spells.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Selling Prayerbooks</strong></summary>

Prayerbooks can be sold for half the total cost of inscribing the spells they contain. Unique or rare spells may be worth more.

</details>

## Dark Knowledge (Su)
At 1st level, the archivist can call upon him exhaustive research and forbidden scholarship to grant himself and him allies mystical insight into the vulnerabilities of unnatural creatures.

As a standard action, the archivist can activate a Dark Knowledge effect, choosing one insight from the list below. This ability affects the archivist and all allies within 30 feet, but only applies against a single creature variant the archivist has identified (e.g., all ghouls, all hell hounds, all azatas). The bonuses do not apply against other creatures, even of the same type (e.g., other undead).

If the archivist has not already identified the target creature variant with a successful Knowledge check, he may attempt the appropriate check as part of activating this ability. If the check fails, the activation attempt fails, no benefits are granted, and 1 minute of him daily Dark Knowledge duration is still consumed. he may attempt to activate Dark Knowledge again on a future turn, consuming another minute as normal.

The archivist can use this ability for a total number of minutes per day equal to him Archivist level + his Intelligence modifier. This duration does not need to be consecutive but must be spent in 1-minute increments. Activating a Dark Knowledge effect cannot be disrupted, but it ends immediately if the archivist is killed, paralyzed, stunned, or rendered unconscious. Only one Dark Knowledge effect may be active at a time.

At 7th level, activating a Dark Knowledge effect becomes a move action. At 13th level, it becomes a swift action. The archivist can change an active effect to another Dark Knowledge effect as a swift action, which consumes a new 1-minute increment and does not require an additional Knowledge check.

- **Tactics:** Allies gain a +1 insight bonus on attack rolls against the creature. This bonus increases by +1 for every five archivist levels he possesses.

- **Puissance:** Allies gain a +1 insight bonus on saving throws against the creature’s abilities. This bonus increases by +1 for every five archivist levels he possesses.

- **Foe:** Allies deal +1d6 precision damage on weapon attacks against the creature. Increases to +2d6 at 6th, +3d6 at 12th, +4d6 at 18th level.

- **Foreknowledge:** Allies gain a +1 insight bonus to AC against attacks from the creature. This bonus increases by +1 for every five archivist levels he possesses.

- **Piercing Insight:** Allies gain a +1 insight bonus on caster level checks made to overcome the creature’s spell resistance. This bonus increases by +1 for every three archivist levels he possesses.

## Scribe Scroll
At 1st level, a Archivist gains Scribe Scroll as a bonus feat.

## Treatise (Ex)
At 1st level, the Archivist selects a single Treatise representing a deep scholarly focus on one particular area of knowledge. This choice is permanent and cannot be changed.

A Treatise grants the Archivist access to a unique list of Treatise Spells related to the chosen subject. These spells are prepared using special Treatise Spell Slots gained alongside the Archivist’s regular spell slots. The Archivist may prepare only spells from the Treatise spell list in these slots.

In addition, the Treatise provides a unique insight that enhances the Archivist’s Dark Knowledge class feature, granting additional insights when invoking Dark Knowledge.

## Sanctioned Heresy (Ex)
At 2nd level, the Archivist gains a pool of esoteric insight points equal to 1/2 his Archivist level + his Intelligence modifier (minimum 1). These points represent the Archivist’s connection to forbidden and unsettling knowledge.

Once per round, as a Free Action, the Archivist can spend 1 point from this pool to invoke Sanctioned Heresy, allowing them to reroll a failed Knowledge check made to identify either a creature, a spell, or a magical effect. The second result must be accepted, even if it is worse.

The Archivist regains all spent esoteric insight points after a full night’s rest.

## Still Mind (Ex)
At 4th level, the Archivist’s intense mental discipline and scholarly detachment provide a measure of defense against mental intrusion. He gains a +2 bonus on saving throws against enchantment spells and effects.

## Archivist Feat
At 6th level, and every six levels thereafter, the Archivist gains a bonus feat in addition to those gained from normal advancement. At each such opportunity, he can choose a Spellcaster Feat, Metamagic Feat, or Item Creation Feat. The Archivist must still meet all prerequisites for a bonus feat, including caster level minimums.

## Improved Dark Knowledge (Ex)
At 8th level, when using his Dark Knowledge feature, the Archivist may select two insights instead of one. The effects apply simultaneously, and the 1-minute increment still applies as normal.

## Greater Dark Knowledge (Ex)
At 16th level, when using his Dark Knowledge feature, the Archivist may select up to three insights. The effects apply simultaneously, and the 1-minute increment still applies as normal.
