        **Arcane Spells:** An Warlock casts arcane spells drawn from the Warlock spell list. 

        **Spontaneous Casting:** Unlike prepared divine casters, an Warlock need not prepare spells in advance and can cast any spell she knows, provided she has spell slots available for that level.

        To learn or cast a spell, an Warlock must have a Charisma score equal to at least 10 + the spell level. The Difficulty Class for a saving throw against an Warlock's spell is 10 + the spell level + the Warlock's Charisma modifier.

        An Warlock can only cast a limited number of spells of each spell level per day, as shown on the Warlock class progression table. She also receives bonus spells per day if his Charisma score is high enough (see Ability Modifiers and Bonus Spells).

        An Warlock's selection of spells is extremely limited. She begins play knowing four 0-level spells of his choice and two 1st-level spells of his choice. At each new Warlock level, she gains one or more new spells, as indicated on the Warlock's Spells Known Table. These new spells can be common spells chosen from the Warlock spell list.

        At 2nd level, and every level thereafter, an Warlock may swap out one spell she knows for another of the same spell level.

        **Cantrips:** Warlocks learn a number of cantrips, or 0-level spells, as noted on the Warlock's Spells Known Table. These spells are cast like any other spell, but they do not consume any slots and may be used again.
        
        <details>
        <summary><strong>Daily Rest and Spell Slot Recovery</strong></summary>

        To recover his spells, an Warlock must meet all the following conditions:

        - **Rest:** The Warlock must rest for 8 hours to recover spell slots. This rest must be free from combat, movement, spellcasting, or other demanding activity. Interruptions add 1 hour per disruption, and she must have at least 1 uninterrupted hour before concentrating for spells.

        - **Concentrating:** After resting, the Warlock spends 15 minutes concentrating to ready his mind to cast spells for the day.

        - **No Recent Casting:** Any spells cast in the last 8 hours count against the number of spells the Warlock can cast per day. For example, casting a 5th-level spell before resting reduces that day’s 5th-level spell slots available to cast by one.
