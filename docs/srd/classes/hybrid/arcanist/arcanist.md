A melding of sorcerer and Arcanist, the arcanist is an arcane tinkerer and spell-twister, reshaping magic to her whims. 

Some spellcasters seek the secrets of magic, pursuing the power to make the impossible possible. Others are born with magic in their blood, commanding unbelievable forces as effortlessly as they breathe. Yet still others seek to meld the science of arcane scholars with the natural might of innate casters. These arcanists seek to discover the mysterious laws of magic and through will and expertise bend those forces to their whims. Arcanists are the shapers and tinkers of the arcane world, and no magic can resist their control.

**Role:** Arcanists are scholars of all things magical. They constantly seek out new forms of magic to discover how they work, and in many cases, to collect the energy of such magic for their own uses. Many arcanists are seen as reckless, more concerned with the potency of magic than the ramifications of unleashing such power.

**Alignment:** Any.

**Hit Die:** d6.

**Starting Wealth:** 2d6 x 10 gp (average 70 gp). In addition, each character begins play with an outfit worth 10 gp or less.

# Class Skills
The Arcanist's class skills are Appraise (Int), Craft (Int), Fly (Dex), Knowledge (all) (Int), Linguistics (Int), Profession (Wis), Spellcraft (Int), and Use Magic Device (Cha).

**Skill Points at each Level:** 2 + Int modifier.

        **Arcane Spells:** An Arcanist casts arcane spells drawn from the sorcerer/wizard spell list.

        **Hybrid Casting:** An Arcanist must choose and prepare her spells ahead of time by resting and studying her spellbook. However, she does not expend individual spells when she casts them. Instead, she can cast any spell she has prepared, using available spell slots of the appropriate level.

        To learn, prepare, or cast a spell, an Arcanist must have an Intelligence score equal to 10 + the spell’s level. The saving throw DC for an Arcanist's spell is 10 + the spell’s level + the Arcanist's Intelligence modifier.

        An Arcanist can only cast a limited number of spells of each spell level per day, as shown on the class progression table. In addition, she receives bonus spells per day if her Intelligence is high enough (see “Ability Modifiers and Bonus Spells”).

        An Arcanist may know any number of spells, but the number she can prepare each day is limited. At 1st level, she can prepare three 0-level spells and one 1st-level spell each day. At each new Arcanist level, the number of spells she can prepare each day increases, adding new spell levels as indicated on the Arcanist's Spells Prepared Table.

        **Cantrips:** Arcanists can prepare a number of cantrips, or 0-level spells, each day, as shown on the class progression table. These spells are cast like any other spell, but they are not expended when cast and may be used again.
        
        <details>
        <summary><strong>Rest and Spell Preparation</strong></summary>

        To prepare spells, an Arcanist must meet all the following conditions:

        - **Rest:** The Arcanist must rest for 8 hours to recover spell slots. This rest must be free from combat, movement, spellcasting, or other demanding activity. Interruptions add 1 hour per disruption, and she must have at least 1 uninterrupted hour before preparing spells.

        - **No Recent Casting:** Any spells cast in the last 8 hours count against the number of spells the Arcanist can prepare. For example, casting a 5th-level spell before resting reduces that day’s 5th-level spell slots available for preparation by one.

        - **Study:** After resting, the Arcanist spends up to 1 hour studying her spellbook to prepare spells. She chooses which spells to prepare and how many times each is prepared, up to her remaining spell slots.

        - **Preparation Environment:** The Arcanists’s surroundings must be reasonably quiet and free of distractions to concentrate. Inclement weather, injury, or failing saving throws during study can prevent preparation. The Arcanist must have access to her spellbook and sufficient light, except she can prepare *read magic* from memory without the spellbook.

        - **Partial Preparation and Open Slots:** The Arcanist can prepare only some spells at a time, requiring at least 15 minutes. She may leave spell slots empty and later prepare spells in those slots by studying for at least 15 minutes. However, she cannot replace or refill slots already used without resting again.

        - **Prepared Spells:** Prepared spells remain in the Arcanist’s mind until cast or abandoned. If the Arcanist dies, all prepared spells are lost.

        </details>

        <details>
        <summary><strong>Spellbook and Gaining Spells</strong></summary>

        A Arcanist must study her spellbook each day to prepare spells. She cannot prepare any spell not recorded in it, except for read magic, which she can prepare from memory.

        - **Starting Spellbook:** At 1st level, the Arcanist’s spellbook contains all 0-level Arcanist spells (except any prohibited schools) plus three 1st-level Arcanist spells of choice, plus additional 1st-level spells equal to her Intelligence modifier.

        - **Gaining New Spells:** Each time the Arcanist gains a new level, she adds two new Arcanist spells of any level she can cast to her spellbook.

        - **Copying Spells:** The Arcanist can copy spells from any prayerbook, spellbook, or spell scroll, as long as the spell is on the Sorcerer/wizard spell list:
          - **Deciphering:** First, deciper the magical writing (Spellcraft DC 20 + spell level, or use *read magic*).
          - **Studying:** Secondly after a successful deciphering, study the spell for 1 hour, then succeeding at a Spellcraft check (DC 15 + spell level). Specialists receive +2 on this check for spells from their specialty school.
          - **Copying:** Thirdly after a successful study, copying the spell to the spellbook, which costs 1 hour per spell level (30 minutes for 0-level spells), one page per spell level, and materials costing gold equal to the spell level’s write cost.

                | Spell Level | Cost |
                |-------------|------|
                | 0           | 5 gp |
                | 1           | 10 gp |
                | 2           | 40 gp |
                | 3           | 90 gp |
                | 4           | 160 gp |
                | 5           | 250 gp |
                | 6           | 360 gp |
                | 7           | 490 gp |
                | 8           | 640 gp |
                | 9           | 810 gp |

        - **Independent Research:** Arcanists may research new spells independently, taking at least 1 week and 1,000 gp per spell level plus relevant Spellcraft and Knowledge (arcana) checks at GM discretion.

        </details>

        <details>
        <summary><strong>Borrowed Spellbooks and Replacing Spellbooks</strong></summary>

        - A Arcanist can prepare spells she already knows from a borrowed spellbook but must:
          - Decipher the writing (Spellcraft DC 20 + spell level, or *read magic*).
          - Succeed on a Spellcraft check (DC 15 + spell level) each time she prepares that spell from the borrowed spellbook.

        - If the Arcanist loses her spellbook, she can reconstruct it by:
          - Writing prepared spells into a new spellbook at the normal cost, erasing them from her mind.
          - Preparing spells from a borrowed spellbook to copy them.
          - Duplicating a spellbook halves the time and cost to copy spells.

        </details>

        <details>
        <summary><strong>Selling Spellbooks</strong></summary>

        A spellbook may be sold for half the total cost of inscribing the spells it contains. Unique or rare spells may be worth more.

        </details>

        <details>
        <summary><strong>Hybrid Metamagic</strong></summary>

        An arcanist may apply metamagic feats in two different ways:

        - **Spontaneous Metamagic:** Like a spontaneous caster, she may apply metamagic feats to a prepared spell as she casts it. A spontaneous metamagic spell uses a higher-level spell slot and increases the casting time (usually to a full-round action for spells with a casting time of 1 standard action).

        - **Prepared Metamagic:** Like a prepared caster, she may also choose to prepare a spell with metamagic effects already applied. A prepared metamagic spell use higher-level spell slots as normal but does not increase the casting time.

        An arcanist cannot combine both methods of metamagic on the same casting. However, the **Metamixing** arcanist exploit allows limited exceptions to this rule.

        </details>
