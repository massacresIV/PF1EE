Combining the natural skills and animal mastery of the druid and the ranger, the hunter teams up with a devoted animal ally to confront the enemies of the wilds.

Hunters are warriors of the wilds that have forged close bonds with trusted animal companions. They focus their tactics on fighting alongside their companion animals as a formidable team of two. Able to cast a wide variety of nature spells and take on the abilities and attributes of beasts, hunters magically improve both themselves and their animal companions.

**Role:** Hunters can adapt their tactics to many kinds of opponents, and cherish their highly trained animal companions. As a team, the hunter and her companion can react to danger with incredible speed, making them excellent scouts, explorers, and saboteurs.

**Alignment:** Any neutral.

**Hit Die:** d8.

**Starting Wealth:** 4d6 x 10 gp (average 140 gp). In addition, each character begins play with an outfit worth 10 gp or less.

# Class Skills
The Hunter's class skills are Climb (Str), Craft (Int), Handle Animal (Cha), Heal (Wis), Intimidate (Cha), Knowledge (dungeoneering) (Int), Knowledge (geography) (Int), Knowledge (nature) (Int), Perception (Wis), Profession (Wis), Ride (Dex), Spellcraft (Int), Stealth (Dex), Survival (Wis), and Swim (Str).

**Skill Points at each Level:** 6 + Int modifier.

        **Divine Spells:** A Hunter casts divine spells drawn from the Druid and Ranger spell lists. spell list. 

        **Spontaneous Casting:** Unlike prepared divine casters, a Hunter need not prepare spells in advance and can cast any spell she knows, provided she has spell slots available for that level.

        Only Druid spells of 6th level and lower and ranger spells are considered to be part of the Hunter spell list. If a spell appears on both the Druid and Ranger spell lists, the hunter uses the lower of the two spell levels listed for the spell. A Hunter cannot use spell completion or spell trigger magic items (without making a successful Use Magic Device check) of Druid spells of 7th level or higher.

        To learn or cast a spell, a Hunter must have a Wisdom score equal to at least 10 + the spell level. The Difficulty Class for a saving throw against a Hunter's spell is 10 + the spell level + the Hunter's Wisdom modifier.

        A Hunter can only cast a limited number of spells of each spell level per day, as shown on the Hunter class progression table. She also receives bonus spells per day if her Wisdom score is high enough (see Ability Modifiers and Bonus Spells).

        A Hunter's selection of spells is extremely limited. She begins play knowing four 0-level spells of her choice and two 1st-level spells of her choice. At each new Hunter level, she gains one or more new spells, as indicated on the Hunter's Spells Known Table. These new spells can be common spells chosen from the Hunter spell list.

        In addition to the spells gained by hunters as they gain levels, each hunter also automatically adds all *summon nature’s ally* spells to her list of spells known. These spells are added as soon as the hunter is capable of casting them.

        At 2nd level, and every level thereafter, a Hunter may swap out one spell she knows for another of the same spell level. She cannot swap any summon nature’s ally spells.

        **Alignment Limits:** Her alignment may restrict her from casting certain spells opposed to her moral or ethical beliefs; see Chaotic, Evil, Good, and Lawful Spells.

        **Orisons:** Hunters learn a number of orisons, or 0-level spells, as noted on the Hunter's Spells Known Table. These spells are cast like any other spell, but they do not consume any slots and may be used again.
        
        <details>
        <summary><strong>Daily Communion and Spell Slot Recovery</strong></summary>

        To recover her spells, an Hunter must meet all the following conditions:

        - **Communion:** The Hunter must perform a daily communion at a fixed time each day. This time is chosen at 1st level, and is typically of personal or spiritual significance—such as the first light of dawn to greet the waking earth, the quiet stillness of midnight when the veil between worlds is thinnest, or the soft glow of twilight that bridges day and night—but may be set at the GM’s discretion. Once selected, this time cannot be changed. If she misses this appointed time, she must perform communion at the next available opportunity or forfeit spell recovery for the day.

        - **Contemplation:** During this communion, the Hunter spends 15 minutes in a state of quiet reflection, attuning herself to the natural world.

        - **No Recent Casting:** Any spells cast in the last 8 hours count against the number of spells the Hunter can cast per day. For example, casting a 5th-level spell before resting reduces that day’s 5th-level spell slots available to cast by one.
