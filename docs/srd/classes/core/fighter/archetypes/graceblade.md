# Graceblade

---
## Weapon and Armor Proficiency
The Graceblade is proficient with simple and martial weapons, as well as light armor and bucklers. 

<u>This replaces the Fighter’s normal weapon and armor proficiencies.</u>

---

## Graceblade's Finesse (Ex)
At 1st level, the Graceblade treats all appropriately sized light or one-handed piercing melee weapons as though they had the finesse weapon quality.

<u>This ability replaces the bonus feats gained at 1st level.</u>

---

## Grace (Ex)
Starting at 2nd level, the Graceblade gains a +1 competence bonus on Reflex saves. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels beyond 2nd.

<u>This ability replaces Bravery.</u>

---

## Insightful Strike (Ex)
At 2nd level, a graceblade becomes adept at focusing her attacks to deal greater damage. When she uses her Dexterity modifier instead of Strength on attack and damage rolls with a weapon possessing the finesse quality, she adds her Intelligence modifier (if any) as a bonus on damage rolls in addition to her Dexterity modifier. This bonus damage does not apply against creatures immune to critical hits or sneak attacks. She cannot benefit from this ability while wearing medium or heavy armor or carrying a medium or heavy load.

<u>This ability replaces the bonus feats gained at 2nd level.</u>

---

## Nimble (Ex)
At 3rd level, a Graceblade gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC while wearing light or no armor. Anything that causes the swashbuckler to lose her Dexterity bonus to AC also causes her to lose this dodge bonus. This bonus increases by 1 for every 4 levels beyond 3rd (to a maximum of +4 at 15th level).

<u>This ability replaces armor training 1, 2, 3, and 4.</u>

---

## Acrobatic Charge (Ex)
At 6th level, a graceblade can charge through difficult terrain and through squares occupied by allies. She must still end her charge in a valid space adjacent to her target. If her movement requires navigating obstacles or hazards, such as leaping from ledges or vaulting over tables, she must attempt appropriate skill checks (typically Acrobatics) to avoid mishaps, as determined by the GM.

<u>This ability replaces the bonus feats gained at 6th level.</u>
