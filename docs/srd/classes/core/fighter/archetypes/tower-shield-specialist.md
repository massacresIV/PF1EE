# Tower Shield Specialist
*Many fighters believe the tower shield is a tool suitable only for troops on the battlefield, claiming it is too large and bulky to use in skirmishes or within dungeon corridors. tower shield specialists defy those notions, using their massive shields with startling skill and incredible effect. They use these seemingly clumsy shields to perform deft maneuvers that confound their enemies.*

:::info[Changes]
The original Tower Shield Specialist gives up all Weapon Training class features, to replicate abilities granted in the Bulwark Style feat line which significantly power crept the archetype.

Given the intervals of specific abilities, you could replicate a majority of the archetypes bonuses at the same and even before the levels you acquire these abilities. It doesn't make sense that a vanilla fighter with the right feat line is a more effective with tower shields than the tower shield specialist. 

As such, this archetype has been rebalanced against Bulwark Style to have earlier feature entry and against Elephant in the Room's Iron Guard feat. It still loses a bonus to Weapon Training and delays access to Advanced Weapon Training options and Gloves of Dueling, balances out the early defensive gains at the cost of early offensive potiental, as opposed to the original archetype which gutted any offensive potential and resulted in the archetype not being used.
:::

## Burst Barrier (Ex)
At 2nd level, a tower shield specialist can use his shield to screen himself from burst spells and effects, gaining a +1 bonus on Reflex saves against them while employing a tower shield. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels after 2nd (to a maximum of +5 at 18th level).

<u>This ability replaces bravery.</u>

---

## Tower Shield Specialist (Ex)
At 2nd level, when a tower shield specialist employs a tower shield in combat, he does not take the –2 penalty on attack rolls because of the shield’s encumbrance.

<u>This ability replaces the bonus feat gained at 2nd level.</u>

---

## Tower Shield Training (Ex)
At 3rd level, a tower shield specialist gains armor training as normal, but while he employs a tower shield, the armor penalty is reduced by 3 and the maximum Dexterity bonus allowed by his armor increases by 2. The benefit increases every four levels thereafter as per standard armor training; if the tower shield specialist is not employing a tower shield, the benefits to armor training revert to the normal bonuses.

---

## Tower Shield Defense (Ex)
At 5th level, while using a tower shield, a tower shield specialist adds his shield bonus (including its enhancement bonus) to his CMD and gains his shield bonus against touch attacks. Additionally, when a Tower Shield Specialist uses a tower shield to gain total cover along one selected edge, he can do so as a Move Action (instead of Standard Action) and he grants any adjacent ally this total cover against any attacks through the selected edge of his space.

<u>This ability replaces Weapon Training 1.</u>

---

## Immediate Repositioning (Ex)
At 6th level, as a Swift or Immediate Action, a tower shield specialist can reposition his tower shield to another edge and gain total cover against a single attack made against him that round. Also, whenever he uses a tower shield to gain total cover along one edge, he (and any adjacent allies) also gain partial cover against spells passing through the selected edge of his space.

<u>This ability replaces the bonus feat gained at 6th level.</u>

## Weapon Training (Ex)
At 9th level, a Tower Shield Specialist gains Weapon Training 1. At 13th level he gains Weapon Training 2, and at 17th level he gains Weapon Training 3.

<u>This ability replaces Weapoon Training 2, 3 and 4.</u>
