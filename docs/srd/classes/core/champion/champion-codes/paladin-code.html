Code of the Paladin

A Champion sworn to the cause of justice and righteousness, the Paladin stands as a beacon of order and virtue against the forces of evil.

Alignment​

  • Lawful Good

Edicts​

  • Obey just laws
  • Defend the innocent
  • Respect legitimate authority

Anathema​

  • Lie for personal gain
  • Harm the innocent or helpless
  • Use evil-aligned magic or consort with fiends

Aura of Good (Ex)​

A Champion who swears the Code of the Paladin radiates a powerful aura of good. This aura functions with the Champion’s Aura class feature and is treated as a good-aligned aura equal to her Champion level. It can be detected by spells such as detect good or similar effects. An Antipaladin who uses smite good on an Paladin deals 2 points of damage per Champion level on the first successful attack.

Detect Evil (Sp)​

A Champion who swears the Code of the Paladin can use detect evil at will, as part of the Champion’s Detect class feature. While using this ability, the Champion can, as a move action, focus on a single creature or object within 60 feet to determine if it is evil, learning the strength of its aura as if having studied it for 3 rounds. While focused in this way, the Champion does not detect evil in other targets within range.

Smite Evil (Su)​

When a Champion who swears the Code of the Paladin uses her Smite ability, it gains the following effects:

  • If the target of the smite is evil-aligned, the Champion adds her Charisma bonus (if any) to her attack rolls and her Champion level to all damage rolls against the target.

  • If the target is an outsider with the evil subtype, an evil-aligned dragon, or an undead creature, the damage bonus on the first successful attack increases to 2 points of damage per Champion level.

  • All attacks against the target automatically bypass any damage reduction the creature might possess.

  • While the smite is active, the Champion gains a deflection bonus to AC equal to her Charisma modifier against attacks made by the target.

  • If the target is not evil-aligned, the smite has no effect, but is still expended for the day.

Code Abilities​

A Champion who swears this Code of the Paladin gains the following Code Abilities as she advances in level.

Divine Grace (Su)​

At 2nd level, a Champion who swears the Code of the Paladin is blessed with divine favor. She adds her Charisma bonus (if any) as a bonus on all saving throws.

Divine Health (Ex)​

At 3rd level, a Champion who swears the Code of the Paladin becomes immune to all diseases, including supernatural and magical diseases such as mummy rot. This is an extraordinary ability.

Code Mantles​

A Champion who swears this Code of the Paladin gains the following Code Mantles as she advances in level.

Mantle of Courage (Su)​

At 3rd level, a Champion who swears the Code of the Paladin exudes a supernatural mantle of courage. She becomes immune to fear (magical or otherwise). Each ally within 10 feet gains a +4 morale bonus on saving throws against fear effects. This mantle functions only while the Champion is conscious, not if she is unconscious or dead.

Mantle of Resolve (Su)​

At 8th level, a Champion who swears the Code of the Paladin is shielded by divine resolve. She becomes immune to charm spells and spell-like abilities. Each ally within 10 feet gains a +4 morale bonus on saving throws against charm effects. This mantle functions only while the Champion is conscious, not if she is unconscious or dead.

Mantle of Faith (Su)​

At 14th level, a Champion who swears the Code of the Paladin radiates a mantle of sanctity. Her weapons are treated as good-aligned for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction. Additionally, any attack made by an ally within 10 feet is treated as good-aligned for the same purpose. This mantle functions only while the Champion is conscious, not if she is unconscious or dead.

Mantle of Righteousness (Su)​

At 17th level, a Champion who swears the Code of the Paladin gains DR 5/evil and becomes immune to compulsion spells and spell-like abilities. Each ally within 10 feet gains a +4 morale bonus on saving throws against compulsion effects. This mantle functions only while the Champion is conscious, not if she is unconscious or dead.

Holy Champion (Su)​

At 20th level, a Champion who swears the Code of the Paladin becomes a living conduit of divine power. Her damage reduction increases to DR 10/evil. Whenever she uses Smite and strikes an evil outsider, that creature is subject to a banishment effect, using her Champion level as the caster level (her weapon and holy symbol automatically count as objects the target hates). After resolving the damage and the banishment, the Smite immediately ends. In addition, whenever she channels positive energy or uses Champion's Touch to heal, she heals the maximum possible amount.
