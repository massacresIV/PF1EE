# Oathbound Champion
While all Champions have their own codes of conduct, either taught by an order, handed down from the gods, or inspired by personal conviction, an Oathbound Champion devotes herself to a singular cause. This devotion grants her additional powers but also binds her to further edicts she must follow. An Oathbound Champion swears an oath to eradicate a certain kind of evil or defend against a particular corruption. This oath grants her access to new spells and alters her typical Champion abilities.

An Oathbound Champion can take multiple oaths, but none of the oaths can replace or alter the same class feature of the Champion as another alternate class feature. For example, a Champion cannot take both the Oath against Corruption and the Oath against the Wyrm because they each replace the aura of courage class feature.

Champions who take up an oath may make a sacred promise to their god or temple to perform some specific and grand action associated with the oath. For example, an Oathbound Champion who takes the Oath of Vengeance may be tasked with killing the orc warlord who razed her home city, while a Champion with the Oath against the Wyrm may be asked to secure a nonaggression pact with a family of dragons. When a Champion completes the sacred promise, the oath is fulfilled, and she may abandon the oath if she so chooses; she may then select another oath, become a standard Champion, or adopt a different Champion archetype.

If a Champion violates the code of her oath, she loses the class abilities associated with that oath until she atones. If she violates her Champion’s code, she loses her oath abilities as well as her other Champion abilities.

---

## Deity
Some oaths are especially compatible with the faiths of certain deities; these deities are listed in each oath description. Other deities may allow these oaths as well, though rarely do deities permit oaths that conflict with their teachings.

## Code of Conduct
The Oathbound Champion must abide by the listed tenets of her oath in addition to the specifics of her deity’s or order’s code of conduct. In some cases, a deity’s or Champion order’s code may conflict with the oath’s tenets; in such cases, the oath is unsuitable for a Champion of that deity or order (for example, the Oath against the Wyrm is incompatible with the teachings of good dragon deities or dragon-riding orders).

## Oath Spells (Su)
An Oathbound Champion’s devotion influences what magic she can perform. At each Champion spell level she can cast, she gains one additional spell slot, called an Oath Slot. This slot must be prepared with one of her Oath Spells.

If the Champion has multiple oaths, the spells from each oath are all added to her Oath Spell list. When preparing an Oath Slot, she may choose any one of her available Oath Spells of that level, similar to how a cleric chooses among multiple domain spells.

This feature functions alongside the Domain Bond option of Champion’s Bond. A Champion who possesses both Oath Slots and Domain Slots prepares and casts them separately, effectively gaining both sets of bonus spell slots in addition to her normal spells.
