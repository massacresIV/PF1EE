# Oath of the Antipaladin
*A Champion sworn to the powers of darkness and destruction, the Antipaladin is an avatar of tyranny, dread, and blasphemy against all that is good and pure.*

---
### Alignment
- Chaotic Evil

### Edicts
- You are dishonorable, dishonest, and committed to breaking the false hopes of kindness.
- You must not bind yourself with any law or tenet beyond those of your Oath and Deity.
- Act dishonorably: lie, cheat, steal, and take advantage of others to get what you want.
- You must seek to destroy or corrupt that which offends you and that which stands in your way, especially the forces of good and law that oppose you.

### Anathema
- Bind yourself to any law, oath, or authority other than your own chaotic will.
- Show mercy, kindness, or compassion without selfish gain.
- Aid the innocent or righteous willingly.
- Obey or uphold any form of justice or order imposed by good or lawful forces.

---

## Aura of Evil (Ex)
A Champion who swears the Oath of the Antipaladin radiates a vile aura of evil. This aura functions with the Champion’s Aura class feature and is treated as an evil-aligned aura equal to her Champion level. A Paladin who uses smite evil on an Antipaladin deals 2 points of damage per Champion level on the first successful attack.

---

## Detect Good (Sp)
A Champion who swears the Oath of the Antipaladin can use *detect good* at will, as part of the Champion’s Detect class feature. While using this ability, the Champion can, as a move action, focus on a single creature or object within 60 feet to determine if it is good, learning the strength of its aura as if having studied it for 3 rounds. While focused in this way, the Champion does not *detect good* in other targets within range.

---

## Smite Good (Su)
When a Champion who swears the Oath of the Antipaladin uses her Smite ability, it gains the following effects:

- If the target of the smite is good-aligned, the Champion adds her Charisma bonus (if any) to her attack rolls and her Champion level to all damage rolls against the target.

- If the target is an outsider with the good subtype, a good-aligned dragon, or a good creature with levels in Cleric, Inquisitor, or Paladin, the damage bonus on the first successful attack increases to 2 points of damage per Champion level.

- All attacks against the target automatically bypass any damage reduction the creature might possess.

- While the smite is active, the Champion gains a deflection bonus to AC equal to her Charisma modifier against attacks made by the target.

- If the target is not good-aligned, the smite has no effect, but is still expended for the day.

---

## Oath Abilities
A Champion who swears this Oath of the Antipaladin gains the following Oath Abilities as she advances in level.

### Unholy Resilience (Su)
At 2nd level, a Champion who swears the Oath of the Antipaladin is infused with dark power. She adds her Charisma bonus (if any) as a bonus on all saving throws.

### Plague Bringer (Ex)
At 3rd level, a Champion who swears the Oath of the Antipaladin becomes a living vessel of disease and filth. She is immune to all diseases, including supernatural and magical diseases such as mummy rot. However, she may still contract and transmit diseases to others.

---

## Oath Mantles
A Champion who swears this Oath of the Antipaladin gains the following Oath Mantles as she advances in level.

### Mantle of Cowardice (Su)
At 3rd level, a Champion who swears the Oath of the Antipaladin exudes an aura of dread. All enemies within 10 feet take a –4 penalty on saving throws against fear effects. Creatures normally immune to fear lose that immunity while within range. This mantle functions only while the Champion is conscious, not if she is unconscious or dead.

### Mantle of Despair (Su)
At 8th level, a Champion who swears the Oath of the Antipaladin weakens the resolve of her enemies. Enemies within 10 feet take a –2 penalty on all saving throws. This penalty does not stack with Mantle of Cowardice. This mantle functions only while the Champion is conscious, not if she is unconscious or dead.

### Mantle of Sin (Su)
At 14th level, a Champion who swears the Oath of the Antipaladin radiates corrupt power. Her weapons are treated as evil-aligned for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction. Additionally, any attack made by an ally within 10 feet is treated as evil-aligned for the same purpose. This mantle functions only while the Champion is conscious, not if she is unconscious or dead.

### Mantle of Depravity (Su)
At 17th level, a Champion who swears the Oath of the Antipaladin gains DR 5/good. Each enemy within 10 feet takes a –4 penalty on saving throws against compulsion effects. This mantle functions only while the Champion is conscious, not if she is unconscious or dead.

### Unholy Champion (Su)
At 20th level, a Champion who swears the Oath of the Antipaladin becomes an unholy conduit of profane power. Her damage reduction increases to DR 10/good. Whenever she uses Smite and strikes a good outsider, that creature is subject to a *banishment* effect, using her Champion level as the caster level (her weapon and unholy symbol automatically count as objects the target hates). After resolving the damage and the banishment, the Smite immediately ends. In addition, whenever she channels negative energy or uses Champion's Touch to harm a creature, she deals the maximum possible amount.
