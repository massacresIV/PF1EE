# Void Domain

# Domain Bonus
*You are a crusader for your god, always ready and willing to fight to defend your faith. In addition, you gain Weapon Focus with a weapon group that includes your deity's favored weapon as a bonus feat.*

:::info[Changes]
Added a Domain Bonus.
:::

# Domain Powers

**Battle Rage (Sp):** As a swift action, you can touch a creature to grant it extra melee damage. For a number of rounds equal to your Wisdom modifier, the target gains an bonus on melee damage rolls equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum +1). You can use this ability three times per day.

:::info[Changes]
Changed from standard action to swift action. Duration increased from 1 round to rounds equal to Wisdom modifier. Reduced uses per day.
:::

**Weapon Master (Su):** At 8th level, you gain a combat feat as a bonus feat. You must meet the prerequisites for this feat, but you treat your Cleric level as your base attack bonus (in addition to base attack bonuses gained from other classes and Hit Dice) for the purpose of qualifying for this feat. Finally, you treat your Cleric level as your Fighter level for the purpose of qualifying for this feat.

:::info[Changes]
Replaced Swift Action temporary combat feat for a permanent warpriest-style combat feat.
:::
