# Weather Domain

# Domain Bonus  
*With power over storm and sky, you can call down the wrath of the gods upon the world below. In addition, you ignore concealment from fog, mist, and precipitation effects against creatures within 5 feet.*

:::info[Changes]
Added a Domain Bonus.
:::

# Domain Powers

**Storm Burst (Sp):** As a standard action, you can create a storm burst targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch attack. The storm burst deals 1d6 points of nonlethal damageincreasing by 1d6 at 3rd level and every 2 cleric levels thereafter (to a maximum of 10d6 at 19th level). You also add your Wisdom modifier to the damage. In addition, the target is buffeted by winds and rain, causing it to take a –2 penalty on attack rolls for 1 round. You can use this ability three times per day.

:::info[Changes]
Improved scaling damage, added ability modifier to damage, reduced uses per day.
:::

**Lightning Lord (Sp):** At 8th level, you can call down a number of bolts of lightning per day equal to your cleric level. You can call down as many bolts as you want with a single standard action, but no creature can be the target of more than one bolt and no two targets can be more than 30 feet apart. This ability otherwise functions as call lightning.

**Lightning Lord (Sp):** At 8th level, you can call down a number of bolts of lightning per day equal to your cleric level. As a standard action, you may call down any number of these bolts, targeting one creature per bolt. No creature can be the target of more than one bolt, and no two targets can be more than 30 feet apart. Each bolt deals 3d6 points of electricity damage + 1 point for every two cleric levels you possess. This ability otherwise functions as *call lightning*.

:::info[Changes]
Added scaling damage to the lightning bolts.
:::
