# Protection Domain

# Domain Bonus
*Your faith is your greatest source of protection, and you can use that faith to defend others. In addition, you receive a +1 bonus on saving throws. This bonus increases by 1 for every 5 levels you possess.*

:::info[Changes]
Changed to untyped bonus.
:::

# Domain Powers

**Resistant Touch (Sp):** As a standard action, you can touch an ally to grant him your Domain Bonus for 1 minute. When you use this ability, you lose your Domain Bonus granted by the Protection domain for 1 minute. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

:::info[Changes]
Language Adjustments.
:::

**Aura of Protection (Su):** At 8th level, you can emit a 30-foot aura of protection for a number of rounds per day equal to your cleric level. You and your allies within this aura gain a +1 deflection bonus to AC and resistance 5 against all elements (acid, cold, electricity, fire, and sonic). The deflection bonus increases by +1 for every four cleric levels you possess beyond 8th. At 14th level, the resistance against all elements increases to 10. These rounds do not need to be consecutive.
