# Death Domain

# Domain Bonus
*You can cause the living to bleed at a touch, and find comfort in the presence of the dead. In addition, you gain a +1 bonus to the saving throw DC of Cleric spells you cast with the Death descriptor.*

:::info[Changes]
Added domain bonus.
:::

# Domain Powers

**Bleeding Touch (Sp):** As a melee touch attack, you can cause a living creature to take 1d6 points of damage per round, increasing by 1d6 at 3rd level and every 2 cleric levels thereafter (to a maximum of 10d6 at 19th level). This effect persists for a number of rounds equal to 1 + your Wisdom modifier or until stopped with a DC 15 Heal check or any spell or effect that heals damage. You can use this ability three times per day.

:::info[Changes]
Improved scaling damage, added ability modifier to round duration, reduced uses per day.
:::

**Death’s Embrace (Ex):** At 4th level, you heal damage instead of taking damage from channeled negative energy. If the channeled negative energy targets undead, you heal hit points just like undead in the area.

:::info[Changes]
Now gained at 4th level instead of 8th level.
:::

# Domain Spells
1st—cause fear, 2nd—death knell, 3rd—animate dead, 4th—death ward, 5th—slay living, 6th—create undead, 7th—destruction, 8th—create greater undead, 9th—wail of the banshee.

## Variant Domain Powers

**Necrotic Tending (Sp):** You can touch an undead creature or area affected by a haunt as a standard action, restoring 1d6 hit points, increasing by 1d6 at 3rd level and every 2 cleric levels thereafter (to a maximum of 10d6 at 19th level). You also add your Wisdom modifier to the hit points restored. This ability has no effect on other creatures. You can use this ability three times per day.

:::info[Changes]
Added scaling damage, added ability modifier to round duration, reduced uses per day.
:::

<u>This replaces the Bleeding Touch domain power of the Death domain.</u>

## Variant Domain Spells
3rd—speak with dead, 6th—antilife shell, 8th—symbol of death.
