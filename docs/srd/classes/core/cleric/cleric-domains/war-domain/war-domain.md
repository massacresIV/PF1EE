# War Domain
**Domain Bonus:** You are a crusader for your god, always ready and willing to fight to defend your faith. In addition, you gain [Weapon Focus](/docs/srd/feats/combat-feats/weapon-focus) as a bonus feat even if you do not meet the prerequisites, but you must choose a weapon group that includes your deity’s favored weapon.
:::info[Changes]
*Added domain bonus.*
:::
# War Domain Powers
**Battle Rage (Sp):** You can touch another creature as a Standard Action, or yourself as a Swift Action, to grant a bonus on melee damage rolls equal to 1/2 your cleric level for 1 round (minimum +1). You can do so a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.
:::info[Changes]
*Added self target Swift Action option.*
:::
**Weapon Master (Su):** At 8th level, you gain a combat feat as a bonus feat. You must meet the prerequisites for this feat, but you treat your Cleric level as your base attack bonus (in addition to base attack bonuses gained from other classes and Hit Dice) for the purpose of qualifying for this feat. Finally, you treat your Cleric level as your Fighter level for the purpose of qualifying for this feat.
:::info[Changes]
*Replaced the old ability with a warpriest-esque combat feat.*
:::
# War Domain Spells
**Domain Spells:** 1st—magic weapon, 2nd—spiritual weapon, 3rd—magic vestment, 4th—divine power, 5th—flame strike, 6th—blade barrier, 7th—power word blind, 8th—power word stun, 9th—power word kill.
