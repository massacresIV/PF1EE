# Magic Domain

# Domain Bonus
*You are a true student of all things mystical, and see divinity in the purity of magic. In addition, can use spell trigger and spell completion items as a wizard of half your cleric level (minimum 1).*

# Domain Powers

**Hand of the Acolyte (Su):** As a swift action, you can empower your next melee weapon attack or melee touch spell or spell-like ability to be delivered at range. Until the end of your turn, you may make one such attack or cast one such spell or ability with a range of 30 feet, rather than requiring melee reach.

When using this ability to make a melee weapon attack, treat it as a ranged attack. Use your Wisdom modifier in place of Dexterity for the attack roll. Damage for this attack is calculated as normal, using whatever ability modifier you would typically apply for that weapon.

When using this ability to cast a melee touch spell or use a melee spell-like ability, treat the attack as a ranged touch attack and use your Wisdom modifier to determine the attack roll.

This ability cannot be used to perform combat maneuvers or to deliver spells that affect multiple targets or create an area of effect. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

:::info[Changes]
Reworked to enable delivery of melee weapon and melee touch spells at range.
:::

**Dispelling Touch (Sp):** At 8th level, you can use a targeted dispel magic effect as a melee touch attack. You can use this ability once per day at 8th level and one additional time per day for every four cleric levels beyond 8th.
