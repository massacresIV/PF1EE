# Plant Domain

# Domain Bonus
*You find solace in the green, can grow defensive thorns, and can communicate with plants. In addition, you gain a +1 natural armor bonus to your AC.*

:::info[Changes]
Added domain bonus.
:::

# Domain Powers

**Wooden Fist (Su):** As a Swift Action, your hands can become as hard as wood, covered in tiny thorns. While you have wooden fists, your unarmed strikes do not provoke attacks of opportunity, deal lethal damage, and you gain the unarmed strikes damage of a monk of your cleric level. This effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum 1). You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

:::info[Changes]
Changed from Free Action to Swift Action. Damage scaling improved to match a monk of equal level. Now has uses per day instead of rounds per day. Duration now flat at 1/2 Cleric level.
:::

**Bramble Armor (Su):** At 6th level, you can cause a host of wooden thorns to burst from your skin as a free action. While bramble armor is in effect, any foe striking you with an unarmed strike or a melee weapon without reach takes 1d6 points of piercing damage, increasing by 1d6 at 3rd level and every 2 cleric levels thereafter (to a maximum of 10d6 at 19th level). You can use this ability for a number of rounds per day equal to your cleric level. These rounds do not need to be consecutive.

:::info[Changes]
Improved scaling damage.
:::
