# Healing Domain

# Domain Bonus
*Your touch staves off pain and death, and your healing magic is particularly vital and potent. In addition, you gain a +2 bonus on Heal checks.*

:::info[Changes]
Added domain bonus.
:::

# Domain Powers

**Rebuke Death (Sp):** You can touch a living creature as a standard action, healing it for 1d6 points of damage, increasing by 1d6 at 3rd level and every 2 cleric levels thereafter (to a maximum of 10d6 at 19th level). You also add your Wisdom modifier to the damage healed. If you use this ability to heal yourself or a living creature below 0 hit points, you may do so as a Swift Action instead of a Standard Action. You can use this ability three times per day.

:::info[Changes]
Can now target all creatures, not just creatures below 0 hit points. Swift Action on self and creatures below 0 hit points. Improved scaling healing, added ability modifier to heal, reduced uses per day.
:::

**Healer's Blessing (Su):** At 6th level, all of your cure spells are treated as if they were empowered, increasing the amount of damage healed by half (+50%). This does not apply to damage dealt to undead with a cure spell. This does not stack with the Empower Spell metamagic feat.

# Domain Spells
1st—cure light wounds, 2nd—cure moderate wounds, 3rd—cure serious wounds, 4th—cure critical wounds, 5th—breath of life, 6th—heal, 7th—regenerate, 8th—mass cure critical wounds, 9th—mass heal.

## Variant Domain Powers

**Exorcise Haunt (Sp):** You can emit a burst of positive energy as a standard action. Any haunt within 15 feet of you takes 1d6 points of damage, increasing by 1d6 at 3rd level and every 2 cleric levels thereafter (to a maximum of 10d6 at 19th level). You also add your Wisdom modifier to the damage. You can use this ability three times per day.

<u>This replaces the Rebuke Death domain power of the Healing domain.</u>

:::info[Changes]
Improved scaling damage, added ability modifier to damage, reduced uses per day.
:::
