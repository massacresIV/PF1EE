# Earth Domain

# Domain Bonus
*You have mastery over earth, metal, and stone, can fire darts of acid, and command earth creatures. In addition, you gain a +1 bonus to your caster level when casting Cleric spells with the acid descriptor or the earth descriptor.*

:::info[Changes]
Added domain bonus.
:::

# Domain Powers

**Acid Dart (Sp):** As a standard action, you can unleash an acid dart targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch attack. This acid dart deals 1d6 points of acid damage, increasing by 1d6 at 3rd level and every 2 cleric levels thereafter (to a maximum of 10d6 at 19th level). You also add your Wisdom modifier to the damage. You can use this ability three times per day.

:::info[Changes]
Improved scaling damage, added ability modifier to damage, reduced uses per day.
:::

**Acid Resistance (Ex):** At 6th level, you gain resist acid 10. This resistance increases to 20 at 12th level and to 30 at 18th level. At 20th level, you gain immunity to Acid.

:::info[Changes]
Improved Scaling and added new resistance intervals.
:::
