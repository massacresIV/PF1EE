# Community Domain

# Domain Bonus
*Your touch can heal wounds, and your presence instills unity and strengthens emotional bonds. In addition, you treat Knowledge (Local) as a class skill.*

:::info[Changes]
Added domain bonus.
:::

# Domain Powers

**Calming Touch (Sp):** You can touch a creature as a standard action to heal it of 1d4 points of damage, increasing by 1d4 at 3rd level and every 2 cleric levels thereafter (to a maximum of 10d4 at 19th level). You also add your Wisdom modifier to the damage healed. This touch also removes the fatigued, shaken, and sickened conditions (but has no effect on more severe conditions). You can use this ability three times per day.

:::info[Changes]
Heals all damage, not just nonlethal. Improved scaling healing, added ability modifier to heal, reduced uses per day.
:::

**Unity (Su):** At 8th level, whenever a spell or effect targets you and one or more allies within 30 feet, you can use this ability to allow your allies to use your saving throw against the effect in place of their own. Each ally must decide individually before the rolls are made. Using this ability is an immediate action. You can use this ability once per day at 8th level, and one additional time per day for every four cleric levels beyond 8th.

## Home Subdomain

### Replacement Power
The following granted power replaces the unity power of the Community domain.

**Guarded Hearth (Su):** At 8th level, you can create a ward that protects a specified area. Creating this ward is a Full-Round Action. This ward has a maximum radius of 5 feet per 2 cleric levels you possess. When the ward is completed, you designate any number of creatures inside its area. Should any other creature enter the warded area, all of the selected creatures are immediately alerted (and awoken if they were asleep). The designated creatures also receive a Sacred Bonus equal to your Wisdom modifier on all saving throws and attack rolls while inside the warded area. This ward immediately ends if you leave the area. The ward lasts for 1 hour per Cleric level. You can use this ability once per day.

:::info[Changes]
Changed from 10-minute casting time to a Full-Round Action.
:::
