# Water Domain

# Domain Bonus
*You can manipulate water and mist and ice, conjure creatures of water, and resist cold. In addition, you gain a +1 bonus to your caster level when casting Cleric spells with the cold descriptor or the water descriptor.*

:::info[Changes]
Added a Domain Bonus.
:::

# Domain Powers

**Icicle (Sp):** As a standard action, you can fire an icicle from your finger, targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch attack. The icicle deals 1d6 points of cold damage, increasing by 1d6 at 3rd level and every 2 cleric levels thereafter (to a maximum of 10d6 at 19th level). You also add your Wisdom modifier to the damage. You can use this ability three times per day.

:::info[Changes]
Improved scaling damage, added ability modifier to damage, reduced uses per day.
:::

**Cold Resistance (Ex):** At 6th level, you gain resist cold 10. This resistance increases to 20 at 12th level. At 20th level, you gain immunity to cold.
