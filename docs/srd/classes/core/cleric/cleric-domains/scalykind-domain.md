# Scalykind Domain

# Domain Bonus
*You are a true lord of reptiles, and your gaze can drive weak creatures into unconsciousness. In addition, you treat Handle Animal as a class skill.*

:::info[Changes]
Added domain bonus.
:::

# Domain Powers

**Venemous Stare (Sp):** As a standard action, you can activate a gaze attack with a 30-foot range. This is an active gaze attack that can target a single creature within range. The target must succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your cleric level + your Wisdom modifier) or take 1d6 points of nonlethal damage. This damage increases by 1d6 at 3rd level and every 2 cleric levels thereafter (to a maximum of 10d6 at 19th level). You also add your Wisdom modifier to the damage dealt.

A creature that fails its saving throw is also fascinated until the beginning of your next turn.  You can use this ability three times per day. This is a mind-affecting effect.

:::info[Changes]
Improved scaling damage, added ability modifier to damage, reduced uses per day.
:::

**Reptilian Companion (Ex):** At 4th level, you gain the service of an animal companion. Your effective druid level for this animal companion is equal to your cleric level –3. You may choose a reptilian animal such as viper or constrictor snake, a lizard, crocodile, or dinosaur, subject to GM approval.

:::info[Changes]
Renamed from Serpentine Familiar. Expanded for a larger selection of reptilian companions. Effective druid level reduced to match Animal Domain.
:::
