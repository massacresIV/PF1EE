# Savage Barbarian
*Some barbarians are truly savage, having little training in modern arms or even an understanding of basic literacy. These savage barbarians learn to avoid blows and toughen up their skin.*

---

## Illiteracy
Savage Barbarians lack the ability to read and write. A Savage Barbarian may spend 2 skill ranks to gain the ability to read and write all languages he is able to speak. A Savage Barbarian who gains a level in any other class automatically gains literacy. Any other character who gains a Savage Barbarian level does not lose the literacy he or she already had.

---

## Naked Courage (Ex)
At 3rd level, the savage barbarian gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC and a +1 morale bonus on saving throws against fear when wearing no armor (shields are allowed). This bonus increases by +1 for every six levels after 3rd.

<u>This ability replaces Danger Sense.</u>

---

## Natural Toughness (Ex)
At 2nd level, when unarmored and unencumbered, the Savage Barbarian adds her Constitution bonus (if any) to her AC and CMD. In addition, a Savage Barbarian gains a +1 bonus to AC and CMD at 4th level. This bonus increases by 1 for every four Savage Barbarian levels thereafter, up to a maximum of +5 at 20th level.

These bonuses to AC apply even against touch attacks or when the Savage Barbarian is flat-footed. She loses these bonuses when she is immobilized or helpless, when she wears any armor, or when she carries a medium or heavy load.

<u>This ability replaces Damage Reduction.</u>
