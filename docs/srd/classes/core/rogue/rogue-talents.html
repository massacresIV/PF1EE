Rogue Talents

Acrobatic Assist (Ex)​

Benefit(s): As a Swift Action, you perform an aid another action to assist an adjacent ally’s Acrobatics check, so long as he makes the check as part of movement that passes through your space or an adjacent space. Additionally, whenever you use the aid another action to assist an ally in making an Acrobatics check and you succeed, your ally gains a +2 dodge bonus to AC against attacks of opportunity caused when he moves out of or within a threatened area, until the end of his turn. She must spend 1 guile point to use this ability.

Changes

Now uses a guile point instead an AOO, removing dependence on Combat Reflexes. Increased dodge bonus from +1 to +2.

Acrobatic Stunt (Ex)​

Skill Stunts: A Rogue may select multiple skill stunts, each keyed to a different Dexterity– or Strength-based skill, but may not select the same one twice.

Prerequisite(s): Trained in Acrobatics.

Benefit(s): When flanked by at least two opponents, a rogue with this talent can, as an Immediate Action when any of the threatening opponents attacks her, attempt an Acrobatics check against the highest CMD of the threatening creatures plus 2 per threatening creature beyond the second. If successful, the rogue may take a 5-foot step in any direction, so long as she remains within the reach of the attacker triggering the movement. The rogue’s final position is used to determine whether the attacker benefits from flanking. On a failed check, the rogue remains in her original position and the attack resolves as normal (the opponent gains a +4 bonus for attacking a prone target). She must spend 1 guile point to use this ability.

Changes

Now uses a guile point instead an once per day. No longer knocked prone on a failed check.

Accurate Poisoner (Ex)​

Benefit(s): When the Rogue successfully hits an opponent with a poisoned weapon and would deal sneak attack damage, she increases the poison’s potency. The poison’s DC increases by 1 for every two sneak attack dice the rogue possesses.

Changes

No longer forgoes damage. Now increases the DC by sneak attack dice rather than a +2 to poison duration.

Armor Piercer (Ex)​

Sneak Attack Talent: A Rogue may select multiple Sneak Attack talents, but only one of these talents can be applied, and only to a single attack made as part of an attack or full-attack action. The Rogue must make this decision before the attack roll is made.

Benefit(s): When you hit with an attack that deals sneak attack damage, you reduce the target’s natural armor bonus by 1 for each die of the rogue’s sneak attack (e.g. 4d6 equals 4 points of natural armor reduction) to a minimum natural armor bonus of +0, lasting until the end of your next turn. A creature cannot be affected by this ability again for 1 minute.

Changes

No longer forgoes damage.

Assault Leader (Ex)​

Benefit(s): When the Rogue misses with a melee attack on a flanked opponent, she can designate a single ally who is flanking the target that her attack missed. That ally can make a single melee attack against the opponent as an Immediate Action. She must spend 2 guile points to use this ability.

Changes

Now uses guile. Now only triggered by a Rogue's melee attack.

Bardic Pretender (Ex)​

Benefit(s): You can grant your Rogue's Edge competence bonus for a chosen skill to all allies within 30 feet for 1 minute. Additionally, the Rogue counts as having the Inspire Competence bardic performance class feature for the purpose of meeting prestige class requirements, although this talent doesn’t grant her the bardic performance ability. She must spend 1 guile point to use this ability.

Changes

Now has an ability.

Befuddling Strike​

Sneak Attack Talent: A Rogue may select multiple Sneak Attack talents, but only one of these talents can be applied, and only to a single attack made as part of an attack or full-attack action. The Rogue must make this decision before the attack roll is made.

Benefit: When the rogue deals sneak attack damage against an opponent, that opponent takes a –2 penalty on attack rolls against the Rogue for a number of rounds equal to half her total amount of Sneak Attack dice (minimum 1).

Changes

Duration scales now scales with Sneak Attack.

Black Market Connections​

Benefit: The rogue gains Black Market Dealings as a bonus feat, even if she does not meet the prerequisites. She treats every settlement as one size greater for the purpose of determining the gp limit of the base value of items for sale, as well as the number of minor, medium, and major magic items for sale in the settlement. If the settlement is already a metropolis, all minor and medium magic items are for sale, as well as 3d8 major magic items.

When she succeeds on a Diplomacy check to locate merchants with deeper pockets in a black market or settlement, she instead treats the settlement as two sizes larger. If the settlement is already a metropolis and she succeeds at the check, all magic items are for sale. If the settlement is already a large city and she succeeds at the check, all minor and medium magic items are for sale, as well as 3d8 major magic items.

Finally, with a successful Diplomacy check, the Rogue can also sell stolen items on the black market. If the check fails by 5 or more, the rogue does something to spook the market, and treats the city as normal for 1 week. Furthermore, those in control of the black market may alert the authorities to the rogue’s presence in an act of reprisal for spooking the market or to divert attention away from their illicit activities. The DCs of the checks are by settlement size and are given in the table.

Changes

Integrated the original ability as an addon to the Black Market Dealings feat.

Bleeding Attack (Ex)​

Sneak Attack Talent: A Rogue may select multiple Sneak Attack talents, but only one of these talents can be applied, and only to a single attack made as part of an attack or full-attack action. The Rogue must make this decision before the attack roll is made.

Benefit: A Rogue with this talent can cause living opponents to bleed by hitting them with a sneak attack. This attack causes the target to take 1 additional point of damage each round for each die of the rogue’s sneak attack (e.g., 4d6 equals 4 points of bleed). Bleeding creatures take that amount of damage every round at the start of each of their turns. The bleeding can be stopped by a successful DC 15 Heal check or the application of any effect that heals hit point damage. Bleed damage from this ability does not stack with itself. Bleed damage bypasses any damage reduction the creature might possess.

Bomber (Su)​

Benefit: A Rogue with this talent can create and throw a bomb by spending 1 point from her guile pool. These bombs function as alchemist’s bombs, except they deal damage equal to the damage dealt by the Rogue’s sneak attack (the Rogue does not add her Intelligence modifier to this damage).

Changes

Now uses the guile pool instead of a fixed number of bombs per day.

Bomber's Discovery (Su)​

Prerequisite(s): Bomber Rogue Talent

Benefits: A Rogue with this talent gains an Alchemist’s discovery that modifies a bomb.

Camouflage (Ex)​

Benefit: A Rogue with this talent can craft simple but effective camouflage from the surrounding materials and foliage. The rogue needs 1 minute to prepare the camouflage, but once she does, it is effective for the rest of the day or until the rogue fails a saving throw against an area-effect spell that deals acid, cold, or fire damage, whichever comes first. The rogue gains a +4 bonus on Stealth checks while within terrain that matches the foliage used to make the camouflage, increasing to +8 when she is attempting to maintain her obscured location. She must spend 1 guile point to use this ability.

Changes

Removed terrain restriction to allow use in urban environments. Doubles the bonus for use in conjunction with Sniping rules. Now uses Guile.

Canny Observer (Ex)​

Benefit: When a rogue with this talent makes gains a +4 bonus on Perception checks.

Changes

Now a flat bonus to Perception.

Card Sharp (Su)​

Benefit: The rogue gains Deadly Dealer as a bonus feat, even if she does not meet the prerequisites. Thrown cards are treated as darts, but the rogue cannot imbue cards with additional power unless she has the Arcane Strike feat. This talent otherwise functions as the Deadly Dealer feat.

Careful Stab (Ex)​

Benefit: A Rogue with this talent takes no penalty on attack rolls for using a lethal weapon to deal nonlethal damage and can use a lethal weapon to deal nonlethal damage with a sneak attack. Additionally, if she reduces a creature to fewer than 0 hp with precision damage, she can choose to leave that creature at -1 hp and stable.

Changes

Added the ability to deal nonlethal damage with lethal weapons and sneak attack.

Castling (Ex)​

A rogue with this talent treats soft cover granted by creatures of her size or larger as though it were cover instead. She can attempt to hide behind creatures of her size and larger, even against creatures aware of her presence and creatures observing her. If the creature she uses for cover is aware of her presence and actively trying to avoid concealing her, she takes the -20 penalty to the Stealth check. On a successful check, all creatures lose track of her presence and treat her as if she had total concealment.

Changes

Added the ability to hide behind creatures whilst being observed.

Certainty (Ex)​

Benefit: The rogue chooses one of the skills she selected with Rogue’s Edge. Her competence bonus to that skill is doubled. Additionally, as an Immediate Action, the Rogue can spend 1 guile point to reroll a skill check she just made with the selected skill and take the better result.

Changes

Doubles the new competence bonus. Now uses guile instead of Once per Day.

Charmer (Ex)​

Benefit: The rogue can roll two dice while making a Diplomacy check, and take the better result. She must choose to use this talent before making the Diplomacy check. She must spend 1 guile point to use this ability.

Changes

Now uses guile instead of Once per Day.

Climbing Stunt (Ex)​

Skill Stunts: A Rogue may select multiple skill stunts, each keyed to a different Dexterity– or Strength-based skill, but may not select the same one twice.

Prerequisite(s): Trained in Acrobatics.

Benefit(s): A rogue with this talent can take a –10 penalty on a Climb check to move at full speed while climbing.

One of Those Faces (Sp)​

The rogue can use disguise self as a spell-like ability for up to 10 minutes per character level. This duration need not be continuous, but it must be used in 10-minute increments. She must spend 1 guile point to use this ability.

Changes

Removed appearance limitation. Now uses guile instead of Once per Day.

  • Ki Pool
