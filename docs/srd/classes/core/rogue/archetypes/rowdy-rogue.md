# Rowdy Rogue

## Weapon and Armor Proficiency
A Rowdy Rogue is proficient with all simple and martial weapons, plus the hand crossbow.

<u>This alters the rogue’s weapon proficiencies.</u>

---
## Vital Force
At 1st level, whenever a Rowdy Rogue successfully uses the Vital Strike feat to make an attack that also deals sneak attack damage, it deals an extra 2d6 precision damage per sneak attack dice.

<u>This ability replaces evasion.</u>

---

## Vital Line
At 1st level, the rowdy gains Vital Strike as a bonus feat.

At 6th level he gains the Improved Vital Strike feat, and at 11th level he gainst the Greater Vital Strike feat. The Rowdy does not need to meet the prerequisites of these feats.

<u>This ability replaces Guile.</u>
