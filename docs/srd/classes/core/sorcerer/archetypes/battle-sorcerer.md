# Battle Sorcerer
*Not all sorcerers are frail spellcasters who rely solely on their magic. Some are warriors who combine arcane power with martial prowess, standing toe-to-toe with their foes.*

---

## Weapon and Armor Proficiency
A Battle Sorcerer is proficient with simple weapons and light armor, and a one-handed martial weapon of her choice (selected at 1st level). She is not proficient with medium or heavy armor, or shields. A Battle Sorcerer can cast Battle Sorcerer spells while wearing light armor without incurring the normal arcane spell failure chance. Like any other arcane spellcaster, a Battle Sorcerer wearing medium or heavy armor incurs a chance of arcane spell failure if the spell in question has a somatic component. A multiclass Battle Sorcerer still incurs the normal arcane spell failure chance for arcane spells received from other classes.

<u>This alters the sorcerer’s weapon and armor proficiencies.</u>

---

## Hit Die and Base Attack Bonus
A Battle Sorcerer uses a d8 Hit Die (instead of d6) and a 3/4 base attack bonus progression (much like a bard’s base attack bonus is calculated), rather than the usual Sorcerer base attack bonus.

<u>This alters the Sorcerer’s Hit Die and base attack bonus.</u>

---

## Reduced Spellcasting
A Battle Sorcerer has fewer daily spell slots than a standard sorcerer. Subtract one spell per day from each spell level on the Sorcerer's class progression table (to a minimum of zero spells per day). For example, a 1st-level Battle Sorcerer may cast four 0-level spells and two 1st-level spells per day (plus bonus spells, if any).

A Battle Sorcerer knows fewer spells per spell level than a standard sorcerer. Subtract one spell known from each spell level on the Sorcerer's Spells Known Table (to a minimum of one spell per spell level). For example, a 5th-level Battle Sorcerer knows five 0-level spells, three 1st-level spells, two 2nd-level spells, and one 3rd-level spell.


<u>This alters the Sorcerer’s Spellcasting.</u>
