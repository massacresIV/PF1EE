Assassin

A mercenary undertaking his task with cold, professional detachment, the assassin is equally adept at espionage, bounty hunting, and terrorism. At his core, an assassin is an artisan, and his medium is death. Trained in a variety of killing techniques, assassins are among the most feared classes.

While nearly any class is capable of becoming an assassin, rogues suit the part more than any other, from both an ability viewpoint and an ideological one. Though they make excellent allies during combat, assassins excel in more clandestine situations, and the best assassins are the ones the victims never knew existed.

Role: Assassins tend to be loners by nature, seeing companions as liabilities at best. Sometimes an assassin’s missions put him in the company of adventurers for long stretches at a time, but few people are comfortable trusting a professional assassin to watch their backs in a fight, and are more likely to let the emotionless killer scout ahead or help prepare ambushes.

Alignment: Due to its necessary selfishness and callous indifference toward taking lives, the assassin class attracts those with evil alignments more than any others. Because the profession requires a degree of self-discipline, chaotic characters are ill suited to becoming these shadowy killers. Neutral characters sometimes become assassins, frequently thinking of themselves as simple professionals performing a job, yet the nature of their duties inevitably pushes them toward an evil alignment.

Hit Die: d8.

Requirements​

To qualify to become an assassin, a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

Alignment: Any evil.

Skills: Disguise 2 ranks, Stealth 5 ranks.

Special: The character must have been hired to kill someone in cold blood, usually for some form of payment.

Class Skills​

The Assassin’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are Acrobatics (Dex), Bluff (Cha), Climb (Str), Diplomacy (Cha), Disable Device (Dex), Disguise (Cha), Escape Artist (Dex), Intimidate (Cha), Linguistics (Int), Perception (Wis), Sense Motive (Wis), Sleight of Hand (Dex), Stealth (Dex), Swim (Str), and Use Magic Device (Cha).

Skill Ranks at Each Level: 4 + Int modifier.

Death Attack (Ex)​

As a full-round action, an assassin can make a single melee attack against a creature affected by their Mark ability. If the attack hits, the target must succeed on a Fortitude save (DC 10 + the assassin’s class level + the assassin’s Intelligence modifier) or die instantly. On a successful save, the target is shaken for 1d4 rounds, and the attack deals sneak attack damage even if the target isn’t flanked or flat-footed. Regardless of the save, the target gains immunity to the assassin’s Death Attack for 24 hours. If the attack misses, no save is required and the target does not gain immunity. This ability cannot be used against creatures immune to critical hits.

Mark (Ex)​

As a move action, an assassin can designate one creature as his mark. he may only have one marked creature at a time; marking a new creature immediately ends any previous mark.

The assassin’s attacks against his marked target ignore damage reduction and immunity to poison. Whenever the assassin deals sneak attack damage to a creature, he may mark that creature as an immediate action.

At 7th level, the assassin can mark a target as a Move or Swift action.

To mark a creature, the assassin must be able to clearly identify the target, such as having seen it in person, studied its likeness, or received an accurate description. he does not need to maintain line of sight or awareness of the target’s location to maintain the mark.

Poison Use (Ex)​

At 1st level, an assassin gains Poison Use as a bonus feat, even if he does not meet the prerequisites.

Sneak Attack​

At 1st level, if an Assassin can catch an opponent when they are unable to defend themselves effectively, he can strike a vital spot for extra damage.

The Assassin’s attack deals extra damage anytime his target would be denied its Dexterity bonus to AC (whether the target actually has a Dexterity bonus or not), or when the target is flanked by the Assassin or his allies. This extra damage is 1d6 at 1st level, and increases by 1d6 every 2 Assassin levels thereafter. Ranged attacks can count as sneak attacks only if the target is within 30 feet. This additional damage is precision damage and is not multiplied on a critical hit.

Additionally, for every two dice of sneak attack damage the Assassin possesses, he gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls made with attacks that deal sneak attack damage.

With a weapon that deals nonlethal damage (such as a sap, unarmed strike, or whip), an Assassin can make a sneak attack that deals nonlethal damage instead of lethal damage. he cannot use a weapon that deals lethal damage to deal nonlethal damage in a sneak attack—not even with the usual –4 penalty.

The Assassin must be able to see the target well enough to pick out a vital spot and must be able to reach such a spot. An Assassin cannot sneak attack while striking a creature with total concealment.

Unified Dice Scaling

Spellcasting​

Beginning at 1st level, an assassin gains the ability to cast a number of arcane spells. To cast a spell, an assassin must have an Intelligence score of at least 10 + the spell’s level, so an assassin with an Intelligence of 10 or lower cannot cast these spells. Assassin bonus spells are based on Intelligence, and saving throws against these spells have a DC of 10 + spell level + the assassin's Intelligence bonus.

At 3rd level and every 3 levels thereafter, an assassin can choose to learn a new spell in place of one he already knows. The new spell’s level must be the same as that of the spell being exchanged. An assassin may swap only a single spell at any given level, and must choose whether or not to swap the spell at the same time that she gains new spells known for the level.

Poison Resistance (Ex)​

At 2nd level, an assassin gains a +1 bonus on saving throws against poison. This bonus increases by 1 for every two assassin levels thereafter (to a maximum of +5 at 8th level). At 10th level, the assassin becomes completely immune to poison.

Uncanny Dodge (Ex)​

At 2nd level, an assassin cannot be caught flat-footed, even if the attacker is invisible. He still loses his Dexterity bonus to AC if immobilized. An assassin with this ability can still lose his Dexterity bonus to AC if an opponent successfully uses the feint action against him.

If an assassin already has uncanny dodge from a different class, he automatically gains improved uncanny dodge instead.

Swift Poisoning (Ex)​

At 3rd level, an assassin can apply a dose of poison to a weapon as a Swift Action.

Hidden Weapons (Ex)​

At 4th level, an assassin becomes a master at hiding weapons on his body. He adds his assassin level to all Sleight of Hand skill checks made to prevent others from noticing them.

True Death (Su)​

Starting at 4th level, anyone slain by an assassin’s death attack becomes more difficult to bring back from the dead. Spellcasters attempting to bring a creature back from the dead using raise dead or similar magic must make a caster level check with a DC equal to 15 + the assassin’s level or the spell fails and the material component is wasted. Casting remove curse the round before attempting to bring the creature back from the dead negates this chance. The DC of the remove curse is 10 + the assassin’s level.

Improved Uncanny Dodge (Ex)​

At 5th level and higher, an assassin can no longer be flanked. This defense denies a rogue the ability to sneak attack the assassin by flanking him, unless the attacker has at least four more rogue levels than the target has assassin levels.

If a character already has uncanny dodge from another class, the levels from the classes that grant uncanny dodge stack to determine the minimum class level required to flank the character.

Quiet Death (Ex)​

At 6th level, whenever an assassin kills a creature using his death attack during a surprise round, he can also make a Stealth check, opposed by Perception checks of those in the vicinity to prevent them from identifying him as the assailant. If successful, those nearby might not even notice that the target is dead for a few moments, allowing the assassin to avoid detection.

Sudden Death​

At 7th level, once per day, the assassin can attempt a Death Attack without having to mark the target beforehand. The assassin must still hit the target with a melee sneak attack to trigger the Death Attack’s Fortitude save. The target is then affected by Death Attack as normal.

Hide in Plain Sight (Su)​

At 8th level, an assassin can use the Stealth skill even while being observed. As long as he is within 10 feet of some sort of shadow, an assassin can hide himself from view in the open without having anything to actually hide behind. He cannot, however, hide in his own shadow.

Perfect Death Attack (Ex)​

At 9th level, the assassin learns to execute their death attacks with terrifying precision. When using their Death Attack ability, the assassin may instead perform the attack as a standard action rather than a full-round action. In addition, if the target succeeds at their Fortitude save, the attack is treated as a critical threat (the assassin must still confirm the critical hit), in addition to dealing sneak attack damage and inflicting the shaken condition as normal.

Angel of Death (Su)​

At 10th level, when the assassin successfully uses Death Attack on a creature that fails its Fortitude save, they may choose to have the creature's body disintegrate into dust as an Free Action. A body destroyed in this way cannot be restored by raise dead or resurrection, though true resurrection or similar effects function normally. The assassin can use this ability once per day.

Evasion (Ex)​

At 11th level, the assassin gains Evasion. If he succeeds on a Reflex saving throw against an attack that normally deals half damage on a successful save, he instead takes no damage. This ability functions only while the assassin is wearing light armor or no armor. A helpless assassin does not gain the benefit of evasion.

If the assassin already has the Evasion ability from another class, he instead gains Improved Evasion. With Improved Evasion, the assassin still takes no damage on a successful Reflex saving throw against attacks, he henceforth takes only half damage on a failed save. A helpless assassin does not gain the benefit of improved evasion.

Relentless Pursuit (EX)​

At 12th level, the assassin’s focus on his marked target sharpens to a supernatural degree. The assassin gains a +2 insight bonus on attack rolls made against any creature currently affected by his Mark ability.

In addition, the assassin can perfectly anticipate the location of his marked target. he ignores all miss chance from concealment and displacement effects against creatures he has marked. This ability does not allow the assassin to see invisible or hidden creatures, nor does it bypass total concealment (such as from solid barriers or complete darkness).

Killer's Mastery (Ex)​

At 13th level, the DC of the assassin’s Death Attack increases by +2. In addition, any poison applied by the assassin also has its save DC increased by +2.

Blood-Chilling Mark (Ex)​

At 14th level, the assassin’s mastery of fear and death allows his to paralyze his enemies through sheer terror at the sight of his handiwork. Whenever the assassin successfully kills a creature using his Death Attack, or reduces a creature to 0 or fewer hit points, he may immediately mark another creature within 30 feet as an immediate action. That creature must succeed at a Will save (DC 10 + the assassin’s class level + the assassin’s Intelligence modifier) or become paralyzed for 1d4 rounds.

A creature that successfully saves is shaken for 1 round instead. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. Whether or not the saving throw is successful, the target is still marked as normal.

Death Incarnate (Ex)​

At 15th level, the Assassin achieves mastery over death itself.

Once per round, the Assassin may attempt a Death Attack (or Perfect Death Attack) as part of any attack they make against a marked target, including attacks made during a full-attack action. This no longer requires expending the usual full-round or standard action to activate Death Attack.

In addition, whenever the Assassin uses Perfect Death Attack and the target succeeds on its saving throw, the resulting critical threat automatically confirms.

The Assassin may have up to two creatures Marked for Death simultaneously.

Finally, the Assassin may expend their Angel of Death ability an additional time per day.
