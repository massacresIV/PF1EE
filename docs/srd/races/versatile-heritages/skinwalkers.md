# Skinwalkers
To play a character with a Skinwalker heritage, first select your race, just like you would for any character. You gain hit points, type, size, speed, languages, heritage traits, and other abilities from that race. Then, instead of choosing a heritage from those normally available to that race, apply your chosen Skinwalker heritage. You gain all the features from your Skinwalker heritage, some of which might modify or replace statistics, abilities, or traits from your race.

**When you apply a Skinwalker heritage, you gain the Skinwalker and Shapechanger subtypes,** while retaining any racial subtypes, creature size, and racial traits associated with your base race (e.g., Dwarf, Elf, Human).

Since a Skinwalker heritage is a heritage, you can only apply one, and you cannot take another heritage in addition to your Skinwalker heritage.

When selecting heritage traits, you can choose from those available to your race as well as those available to your Skinwalker heritage.
