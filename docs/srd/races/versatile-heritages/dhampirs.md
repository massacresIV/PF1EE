# Dhampirs
To play a character with a Dhampir heritage, first select your race, just like you would for any character. You gain hit points, type, size, speed, languages, heritage traits, and other abilities from that race. Then, instead of choosing a heritage from those normally available to that race, apply your chosen Dhampir heritage. You gain all the features from your Dhampir heritage, some of which might modify or replace statistics, abilities, or traits from your race.

**When you apply a Dhampir heritage, you gain the Dhampir subtype,** while retaining any racial subtypes, creature size, and racial traits associated with your base race (e.g., Dwarf, Elf, Human).

Since a Dhampir heritage is a heritage, you can only apply one, and you cannot take another heritage in addition to your Dhampir heritage.

When selecting heritage traits, you can choose from those available to your race as well as those available to your Dhampir heritage.
