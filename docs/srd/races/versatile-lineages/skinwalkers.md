# Skinwalkers
To play a character with a Skinwalker lineage, first select your race, just like you would for any character. You gain hit points, type, size, speed, languages, racial traits, and other abilities from that race. Then, instead of choosing a lineage from those normally available to that race, apply your chosen Skinwalker lineage. You gain all the features from your Skinwalker lineage, some of which might modify or replace statistics, abilities, or traits from your race.

**When you apply a Skinwalker lineage, you gain the Skinwalker and Shapechanger subtypes,** while retaining any racial subtypes, creature size, and racial traits associated with your base race (e.g., Dwarf, Elf, Human).

Since a Skinwalker lineage is a lineage, you can only apply one, and you cannot take another lineage in addition to your Skinwalker lineage.

When selecting racial traits, you can choose from those available to your race as well as those available to your Skinwalker lineage.

## Skinwalker Lineages

### Werebat-kin
Born of shadow and screeching winds, Werebat-kin dwell in darkness both literal and symbolic. With oversized ears and keen awareness, they thrive in twilight and subterranean places, where others stumble blind. Their unsettling presence and erratic energy often set them apart from kin and kind alike. Werebat-kin gain the following racial traits:

- **Change Shape (3 RP):** A Werebat-kin can change shape to and from a hybrid form as a Standard Action. While in this form, the werebat-kin also takes on a bestial feature. This is a polymorph effect. Each time the werebat-kin assumes a hybrid form, they can choose to gain one of the following bestial features:
    - *Bite: The werebat-kin gains a bite attack that deals 1d6 points of damage (or 1d4 if Small). This is a primary natural attack.*
    - *Blindsense: The werebat-kin gains Blindsense to a range of 20 feet.*
    - *Moonlight Descent: The werebat-kin treats all fall damage as 20 feet less than it actually is.*
    - *Moonlit Vigil: The werebat-kin gains a +4 racial bonus on perception checks.*
- **Bat’s Whisper (1 RP):** Werebat-kin can cast *Keen Senses* once per day, using their total character level as caster level. The DC for the spell-like ability is equal to 10 + the spell’s level + the Werebat-kin’s Charisma modifier.

## Skinwalker Racial Traits
At 1st level, you gain one racial trait, and you gain an additional racial trait every 4 levels thereafter (at 5th, 9th, 13th, and 17th levels). As a Skinwalker, you may select from the racial traits available to your base race, as well as those listed below.
