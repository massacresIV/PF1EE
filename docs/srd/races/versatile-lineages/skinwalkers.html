Skinwalkers

To play a character with a Skinwalker lineage, first select your race, just like you would for any character. You gain hit points, type, size, speed, languages, racial traits, and other abilities from that race. Then, instead of choosing a lineage from those normally available to that race, apply your chosen Skinwalker lineage. You gain all the features from your Skinwalker lineage, some of which might modify or replace statistics, abilities, or traits from your race.

When you apply a Skinwalker lineage, you gain the Skinwalker and Shapechanger subtypes, while retaining any racial subtypes, creature size, and racial traits associated with your base race (e.g., Dwarf, Elf, Human).

Since a Skinwalker lineage is a lineage, you can only apply one, and you cannot take another lineage in addition to your Skinwalker lineage.

When selecting racial traits, you can choose from those available to your race as well as those available to your Skinwalker lineage.

Skinwalker Lineages​

Werebat-kin​

Born of shadow and screeching winds, Werebat-kin dwell in darkness both literal and symbolic. With oversized ears and keen awareness, they thrive in twilight and subterranean places, where others stumble blind. Their unsettling presence and erratic energy often set them apart from kin and kind alike. Werebat-kin gain the following racial traits:

  • Change Shape (3 RP): A Werebat-kin can change shape to and from a hybrid form as a Standard Action. While in this form, the werebat-kin also takes on a bestial feature. This is a polymorph effect. Each time the werebat-kin assumes a hybrid form, they can choose to gain one of the following bestial features:
    • Bite: The werebat-kin gains a bite attack that deals 1d6 points of damage (or 1d4 if Small). This is a primary natural attack.
    • Blindsense: The werebat-kin gains Blindsense to a range of 20 feet.
    • Moonlight Descent: The werebat-kin treats all fall damage as 20 feet less than it actually is.
    • Moonlit Vigil: The werebat-kin gains a +4 racial bonus on perception checks.
  • Bat’s Whisper (1 RP): Werebat-kin can cast Keen Senses once per day, using their total character level as caster level. The DC for the spell-like ability is equal to 10 + the spell’s level + the Werebat-kin’s Charisma modifier.

Werebear-kin​

Stalwart and brooding, Werebear-kin embody raw endurance and deep-seated strength. Their quiet watchfulness and sudden ferocity echo the solitary guardians of the forest. When roused to anger, they fight with unstoppable momentum and primal might. Werebear-kin gain the following racial traits:

  • Change Shape (3 RP): A Werebear-kin can change shape to and from a hybrid form as a Standard Action. While in this form, the werebear-kin also takes on a bestial feature. This is a polymorph effect. Each time the werebear-kin assumes a hybrid form, they can choose to gain one of the following bestial features:
    • Bite: The werebear-kin gains a bite attack that deals 1d6 points of damage (or 1d4 if Small). This is a primary natural attack.
    • Claws: The werebear-kin gains two claw attacks that deals 1d4 points of damage (or 1d3 if Small). These are primary natural attacks.
    • Scent: The werebear-kin gains the scent special ability.
    • Tree Climber: The werebear-kin gains a climb speed of 20 feet.
  • Enduring Hide (1 RP): Werebear-kin can cast Endure Elements once per day, using their total character level as caster level. The DC for the spell-like ability is equal to 10 + the spell’s level + the Werebear-kin’s Charisma modifier.

Wereboar-kin​

Brutish and unyielding, Wereboar-kin are driven by a powerful instinct to charge headlong into any threat. Fierce, stubborn, and quick to wrath, they are difficult to reason with once their blood is up—and even harder to stop. Wereboar-kin gain the following racial traits:

  • Change Shape (3 RP): A Wereboar-kin can change shape to and from a hybrid form as a Standard Action. While in this form, the wereboar-kin also takes on a bestial feature. This is a polymorph effect. Each time the wereboar-kin assumes a hybrid form, they can choose to gain one of the following bestial features:
    • Hooves: The werebear-kin gains two hoof attacks that deals 1d4 points of damage (or 1d3 if Small). These are primary natural attacks.
    • Gore: The wereboar-kin gains a gore attack that deals 1d6 points of damage (or 1d4 if Small). This is a primary natural attack.
    • Scent: The wereboar-kin gains the scent special ability.
    • Stampede: The wereboar-kin gains a +10 foot racial bonus to base speed.
  • Boar’s Tenacity (1 RP): Wereboar-kin can cast Longstrider once per day, using their total character level as caster level. The DC for the spell-like ability is equal to 10 + the spell’s level + the Wereboar-kin’s Charisma modifier.

Silent and cold-eyed, Werecrocodile-kin exude patience and menace. Lurking at the edges of society, they are creatures of ambush and endurance, striking only when assured of their advantage. Their powerful jaws and armor-thick skin echo ancient, aquatic predators. Werecrocodile-kin gain the following racial traits:

  • Change Shape (3 RP): A Werecrocodile-kin can change shape to and from a hybrid form as a Standard Action. While in this form, the werecrocodile-kin also takes on a bestial feature. This is a polymorph effect. Each time the werecrocodile-kin assumes a hybrid form, they can choose to gain one of the following bestial features:
    • Bite: The werecrocodile-kin gains a bite attack that deals 1d6 points of damage (or 1d4 if Small). This is a primary natural attack.
    • Natural Armor: The werecrocodile-kin gains a +1 racial bonus to natural armor.
    • Marshstalker: The werecrocodile-kin gains a swim speed of 30 feet.
    • Tail: The werecrocodile-kin gains a tail attack that deals 1d6 points of damage (or 1d4 if Small). This is a primary natural attack.
  • Reptile’s Vigil (1 RP): Werecrocodile-kin can cast Air Bubble once per day, using their total character level as caster level. The DC for the spell-like ability is equal to 10 + the spell’s level + the Werecrocodile-kin’s Charisma modifier.

Wereraptor-kin​

Swift and cunning, Wereraptor-kin combine the primal instincts of predators with coordinated pack tactics. They are light on their feet and quicker to react than most, favoring speed, agility, and precision over brute force. Wereraptor-kin gain the following racial traits:

  • Change Shape (3 RP): A Wereraptor-kin can change shape to and from a hybrid form as a Standard Action. While in this form, the wereraptor-kin also takes on a bestial feature. This is a polymorph effect. Each time the wereraptor-kin assumes a hybrid form, they can choose to gain one of the following bestial features:

    • Hunter's Eye: The wereraptor-kin gains a +4 racial bonus on perception checks.

  • Change Shape (3 RP): A Wererat-kin can change shape to and from a hybrid form as a Standard Action. While in this form, the wererat-kin also takes on a bestial feature. Wereshark-kin​

Ferocious and alien, Wereshark-kin carry the ocean’s cold hunger in their hearts. They are creatures of instinctive violence and uncaring depth, driven by currents both literal and emotional. Their gory rages are feared even among other Skinwalkers. Wereshark-kin gain the following racial traits:

  • Change Shape (3 RP): A Wereshark-kin can change shape to and from a hybrid form as a Standard Action. While in this form, the wereshark-kin also takes on a bestial feature. This is a polymorph effect. Each time the wereshark-kin assumes a hybrid form, they can choose to gain one of the following bestial features:

Proud, graceful, and lethal, Weretiger-kin embody feline nobility tempered by savage power. They move with unsettling silence and strike with devastating force. Solitary by nature and enigmatic in motive, they are apex predators by birthright. Weretiger-kin gain the following racial traits:

Their senses are sharp, their presence primal, and their tempers quick. Whether drawn to the wilderness or the hunt, they are haunted by a relentless drive to protect or to prey. Werewolf-kin gain the following racial traits:

Skinwalker Racial Traits​

At 1st level, you gain one racial trait, and you gain an additional racial trait every 4 levels thereafter (at 5th, 9th, 13th, and 17th levels). As a Skinwalker, you may select from the racial traits available to your base race, as well as those listed below.
