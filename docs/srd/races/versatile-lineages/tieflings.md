# Tieflings
To play a character with a Tiefling lineage, first select your race, just like you would for any character. You gain hit points, type, size, speed, languages, racial traits, and other abilities from that race. Then, instead of choosing a lineage from those normally available to that race, apply your chosen Tiefling lineage. You gain all the features from your Tiefling lineage, some of which might modify or replace statistics, abilities, or traits from your race.

**When you apply a Tiefling lineage, you gain the Tiefling subtype,** while retaining any racial subtypes, creature size, and racial traits associated with your base race (e.g., Dwarf, Elf, Human).

Since a Tiefling lineage is a lineage, you can only apply one, and you cannot take another lineage in addition to your Tiefling lineage.

When selecting racial traits, you can choose from those available to your race as well as those available to your Tiefling lineage.

## Devil-Spawn

- **Forked-Tongue (1 RP)**: Devil-Spawn gain a +1 racial bonus on Diplomacy and Sense Motive checks.
- **Hellish Magic (2 RP)**: Devil-Spawn can use Scorching Ray once per day as a spell-like ability, using their total character level as caster level.
- **Infernal Resistance (1 RP):** Devil-Spawn have Fire resistance 5.
