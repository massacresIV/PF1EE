# Geniekinds

---

## Geniekind Details
To play a character with a Geniekind lineage, first select your race, just like you would for any character. You gain hit points, type, size, speed, languages, racial traits, and other abilities from that race. Then, instead of choosing a lineage from those normally available to that race, apply your chosen Geniekind lineage. You gain all the features from your Geniekind lineage, some of which might modify or replace statistics, abilities, or traits from your race.

**When you apply a Geniekind lineage, you gain the Geniekind subtype,** while retaining any racial subtypes, creature size, and racial traits associated with your base race (e.g., Dwarf, Elf, Human).

Since a Geniekind lineage is a lineage, you can only apply one, and you cannot take another lineage in addition to your Geniekind lineage.

When selecting racial traits, you can choose from those available to your race as well as those available to your Geniekind lineage.
